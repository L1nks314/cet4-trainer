新闻二。
News report 2.
为将来把宇航员送上火星做准备，美国航天局于周五开始招募 4 人，让他们在火星沙丘阿尔法中生活一年。

NASA is planning three experiments with the first one starting in the fall next year.

He said the participants need to be super competent, resourceful and not rely on other people to feel comfortable.

他请她把奖牌挂到自己脖子上。

然后河村就咬了上去。
Kawamura then bit into it.
在记者和摄影师面前咬奖牌，已成为奥运奖牌得主的常见姿势。
Biting a medal in front of journalists and photographers has become a common pose for Olympic medalists.
然而，这只是奖牌得主本人才能做的，别人不行。
However, it is only for the winners themselves, not others.
我真的很抱歉，我弄坏了这位金牌得主的宝贝，河村周四告诉记者。
I'm really sorry that I hurt the treasure of the gold medalist, Kawamura told reporters Thursday.
这位市长说奖牌并未损坏，不过他提出愿意支付换一枚新奖牌的费用。
The mayor said the medal was undamaged, though he offered to pay for the cost of a new one.
不过，据日本媒体报道，后藤已经接受了国际奥委会提出的更换奖牌的建议。
Goto, however, has accepted the International Olympic Committee's offer of a replacement, according to Japanese media reports.
电视上播出的这一幕，引发了数千起向市政厅的投诉。
The scene broadcast on television prompted thousands of complaints to City Hall.
一些奥运选手说，他们把自己的奖牌当作珍宝，河村咬奖牌的做法既不尊重人，也令人无法接受。
Some Olympians said they treat their medals as treasures and that it was disrespectful and unacceptable for Kawamura to bite one.
如果这种事发生在我身上，我会哭的，另一位运动员高藤直寿说。
I would cry if that happened to me, said another athlete, Naohisa Takato.
我拿自己的金牌时非常小心，绝不会让它留下划痕。
I handle my own gold medal so gently that I would not scratch it.
