短文一。
Passage 1.
支持者称之为野外露营。
Supporters call it wild camping.
反对者称之为非法露营。
Opponents call it illegal camping.
双方都承认的是，过去几个月里这股风潮兴起，越来越多的游客在英国任何他们看中的土地上搭起帐篷。
What both sides accept is that there has been a boom in the past few months, with increasing numbers of visitors pitching their tents on any bit of land they fancy in the UK.
这在一定程度上反映出，在出国人数较少的这个夏天，官方营地已全部或部分关闭，或者人满为患。
In part, this reflects the fact that official campsites have been wholly or partially closed or are overflowing, in a summer when fewer people are going abroad.
在许多人都担忧经济前景的时候，这样也很省钱。
It is also cheap at a time when many are worried about what the economic future holds.
但它也可能是人们渴望走进户外的一种表现，是对数月封锁的一种回应。
But it may also be an expression of a desire for going outdoors, a response to the months of lockdown.
关于野外露营风潮的报道大多是负面的。
Most of the coverage of the boom in wild camping has been negative.
如今公共公园在 8 月和 9 月上旬已禁止露营，因为露营者把垃圾、人类排泄物甚至帐篷都丢在草地上。
Camping in public parks has now been banned for August and the early part of September, because campers dump litter, human waste and even their tents on the grassland.
甚至在通常允许在大部分开阔土地上露营的苏格兰，也采取了类似的措施。
Similar action has been taken even in Scotland, where camping is usually permitted on most of its open land.
显然，必须有规则。
Clearly, there have to be rules.
野外露营者需要向土地所有者请求许可才能扎营，这是合情合理的，尤其是在法律限制严格得多的苏格兰以外地区。
It would make sense that wild campers need to ask for permission to camp from landowners, especially outside Scotland, where the law is far more restrictive.
人们使用小帐篷、不留下到访痕迹，这本是常理。
It would be common sense for people to use small tents and leave no trace of their visit.
他们是被一片接近荒野的土地所吸引，而让这片土地保持原样是他们的责任。
They have been attracted by a patch of land that is close to wilderness, and it is their responsibility to keep it that way.
短文二。
Passage 2.
想象一下，你乘船沿亚马逊河顺流而下，专心做自己的事，平静地留意着大得吓人的蛇，这时一只好奇的粉色海豚出现在船边游着。
Imagine boating down the Amazon River, minding your own business, calmly keeping an eye out for alarmingly large snakes, and a curious pink dolphin appears to swim alongside.
这虽然看起来像是神话中的生物，但粉色海豚确实存在于亚马逊地区。
While this may seem like a mythical creature, pink dolphins do exist in the Amazon region.
亚马逊河豚是同类中的庞然大物。
The Amazon River dolphin is a giant among its species.
它体长可达 2 米，体重约 204 公斤。
It can measure up to 2 meters long and weigh around 204 kilograms.
体型并不是亚马逊河豚与众不同的唯一之处。
Size isn't the only thing that sets the Amazon River dolphin apart.
这种淡水海豚在南美河流和季节性洪水形成的临时湖泊中繁衍生息，有时呈现惊人的粉色。
Thriving in South American rivers and temporary lakes caused by seasonal flooding, this freshwater dolphin is sometimes shockingly pink.
该物种的雄性虽出生时是灰色的，但成年后很容易通过明显偏粉的色泽辨认出来。
Although born gray, males of the species are easily identified as they enter adulthood by a decisive pink shade.
它们与众不同的体色被认为是海豚之间争斗留下的疤痕组织所致，无论是嬉戏打闹还是为争夺配偶的认真较量。
Their unusual coloring is believed to be the result of scar tissue from dolphin fights, whether play fighting or a serious bid for a mate.
人们认为颜色越深的粉色代表雄性越有吸引力，而雄性越年长，身上的粉色就越多。
The deeper the pink, the more attractive the males are believed to be, and the older the male, the more pink he will have.
还有一种理论认为，这种颜色有助于海豚更容易融入周围环境。
There's also a theory that this color helps the dolphins more readily blend in with their surroundings.
在大雨期间，亚马逊雨林沿线的河流会呈现粉色，而凭借粉色的体色，雄性海豚更难被发现。
During heavy rains, rivers along the Amazon rainforest turn a pink shade, and with their pink coloring, male dolphins are harder to detect.
由亚马逊河补给的亚马逊湿地系统是粉色海豚繁育的关键场所，自 2018 年起已被授予国际保护地位。
The Amazon wetland system, fed by the Amazon River, is a crucial place for pink dolphins to breed, and since 2018 has been granted internationally protected status.
短文三。
Passage 3.
在美林和 Age Wave 的一项新调查中，整整 70% 的年轻人表示过去一年里得到过父母的经济支持，58% 的人表示没有这些支持他们就负担不起目前的生活方式。
In a new Merrill Lynch Age Wave survey, a full 70% of the early adults said they've received financial support from their parents in the past year, and 58% said they couldn't afford their current lifestyles without it.
最常见的经济支持类型包括手机套餐、食物、学费和用车开支。
The most common types of financial support include cell phone plans, food, school costs, and car expenses.
Age Wave 首席执行官肯·迪希特瓦尔德说，父母为年轻人提供经济支持已成为新常态。
Parental financial support of early adults, said Ken Dichtwald, CEO of Age Wave, is the new normal.
但受访的年轻人中有 64% 表示，父母为 25 到 34 岁的子女提供经济支持是件坏事，因为这会让这些孩子产生依赖。
But 64% of the young adults surveyed said parents' financial support to children aged 25 to 34 is a bad thing, because it makes those kids dependent.
相比之下，只有 29% 的人认为资助 18 到 24 岁的年轻人是坏事。
By contrast, only 29% thought supporting men and women aged 18 to 24 is bad.
其余 71% 的人认为这种资助有助于成年子女取得成功。
The remaining 71% thought that assistance helps the adult children get ahead.
迪希特瓦尔德认为，受访的年轻男女是在说，到了 25 岁，年轻人就应该实现经济独立。
Dichtwald believes the young women and men surveyed were saying that by 25, young adults ought to be financially independent.
事实上，受访者表示，经济独立才标志着成年。
In fact, the respondents said, financial independence defines adulthood.
经济独立正是他们苦苦挣扎、深感挑战的事情。
Financial independence is something they were struggling with and challenged by.
迪希特瓦尔德说，这也让他们有些害怕。
And it scared them a bit, Dichtwald said.
他们苦苦挣扎的一大原因被归咎于助学贷款，其平均金额达到 3.7 万美元。
One big reason they're struggling is attributed to college loans, of which the average amounts to $37,000.
许多父母也为孩子背上了助学贷款，有时甚至牺牲了自己的财务状况。
Many of the parents have taken on college loans for the kids too, sometimes at the expense of their own finances.
调查中，60% 的年轻人把经济上的成功定义为没有债务。
In the survey, 60% of early adults define financial success as being debt-free.
这种情况是否可能、甚至短期内能否实现，谁也说不准。
Whether that's likely or even possible any time soon is anyone's guess.
