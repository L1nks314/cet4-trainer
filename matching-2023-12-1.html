题型：给下面 10 个句子各配一个段落字母（A–L）。 先读句子划关键词，再回原文定位。原文见左侧，题目在右侧。

每周锻炼10分钟延长寿命

12 段 · 10 题
A

Earlier this year, scientists warned that one in six animal species could go extinct(灭绝的) due to climate change. Could the same thing happen to our crops and other foodstuffs too?

今年早些时候，科学家警告说，由于气候变化，六分之一的灭绝的动物物种可能会灭绝。我们的农作物和其他食品也会发生同样的事情吗？

B

It's clear that farmers in many parts of the world are going to find things harder in the coming decades. Last week, BBC Future explored one scientist's efforts to help crops cope with the increased probability of droughts. By using the genes from resurrection(复活) plants, Jill Farrant of the University of Cape Town is exploring whether she can design crops to survive for much longer periods without water.

很明显，在未来几十年里，世界许多地方的农民会发现事情变得更加困难。上周，英国广播公司未来频道探讨了一位科学家帮助作物应对干旱可能性增加的努力。开普敦大学（ University of Cape Town复活 ）的吉尔·法兰特（ Jill Farrant ）正在利用复活（复活）植物的基因，探索她是否可以设计出在没有水的情况下能存活更长时间的作物。

C

But if we can't find ways to protect other foods, will they survive climate change? Fortunately, there is some good news on this front. Despite alarmist headlines about "foods that are going extinct," there is no evidence that major food types like beans, chocolate, wine, corn or wheat will cease to exist.

但是，如果我们不能找到保护其他食物的方法，它们能否在气候变化中幸存下来？幸运的是，这方面有一些好消息。尽管有关“即将灭绝的食物”的危言耸听的头条新闻，但没有证据表明豆类、巧克力、葡萄酒、玉米或小麦等主要食物类型将不复存在。

D

But that doesn't mean it's all good news for future food. We will probably have to change where we grow certain crops, as some regions get too hot. The disadvantage, obviously, is that local farmers will suffer under this situation. And some people may struggle to get the same access to certain foods. "Even if overall food production may be unaffected, food security can still be impacted," says Margaret Walsh, a scientist at the US Department of Agriculture's Climate Change Program Office. In other words, even if a certain food is still grown on some corner of the Earth, it doesn't mean that everyone will continue to have the same degree of access as today.

但这并不意味着这对未来的食物来说都是好消息。由于某些地区变得太热，我们可能不得不改变种植某些作物的地方。显然，缺点是当地农民在这种情况下会受到影响。有些人可能很难获得相同的食物。美国农业部气候变化项目办公室的科学家玛格丽特·沃尔什（ Margaret Walsh ）说： “即使整体粮食生产不受影响，粮食安全仍可能受到影响。”换句话说，即使某种食物仍然在地球的某个角落种植，这并不意味着每个人都能继续像今天一样获得食物。

E

Overall, the yields of many foods, from staples to life-enhancing extras such as coffee and chocolate, will likely be impacted by climate change too. How those decreases will be felt will depend on the degree of warming and the crop in question, but in general, "anything over about 30℃ is very bad for crops," says Wolfram Schlenker, an associate professor of international and public affairs at Columbia University. For example, statistical studies that he and a colleague built of corn and soybean(大豆) production in the US show a steep decline after crossing the 30℃ temperature threshold(临界点).

总体而言，从主食到咖啡和巧克力等提升生活质量的食品，许多食品的产量也可能受到气候变化的影响。哥伦比亚大学国际和公共事务副教授沃尔夫拉姆·施伦克尔（ Wolfram Schlenker ）说，如何感受到这些减少将取决于气候变暖的程度和所涉及的作物，但总的来说， “任何超过30 ℃的东西都对作物非常不利”。例如，他和一位同事在美国建立的玉米和大豆（大豆 ）产量的统计研究表明，在超过30 ℃的温度阈值后，产量急剧下降（临界点 ）。

F

In the US—the world's largest producer of corn and soybeans—farms can move north to some degree, Schlenker says. But eventually, yields will likely suffer because the soil north of Iowa declines in quality—a legacy of glacial(冰川的) expansion. Other studies, including studies of wheat in India and corn in Africa, also found that there is a threshold above which yields sharply decline: crops can adapt and move, but only to a point. "What's common to all studies is the finding that extreme heat is damaging to crop growth, although exact cutoffs vary by crop," Schlenker says. "If predictions for the end of the century are true, though, I think a lot of agricultural areas in the US will see significant hits."

Schlenker说，在美国--世界上最大的玉米和大豆生产国--农场可以在某种程度上向北迁移。但最终，产量可能会受到影响，因为爱荷华州北部的土壤质量下降-这是冰川扩冰川的张的遗产。其他研究，包括对印度小麦和非洲玉米的研究，也发现有一个阈值以上的产量急剧下降：作物可以适应和移动，但只能到一个点。Schlenker说： “所有研究的共同点是，极端高温会损害作物生长，尽管确切的截止日期因作物而异。”“不过，如果对本世纪末的预测属实，我认为美国的许多农业地区将受到重大打击。”

G

Under current conditions, about 4% of the world's croplands experience drought in any given year, but by the end of the century those conditions are forecasted to jump to about 18% per year. Some studies indicate that horticulture crops—generally, everything besides staples—may be impacted most severely, largely because they tend to be confined to a smaller geographic area. Researcher Andrew Jarvis and his colleagues found that 80% of coffee-growing zones in Central America and Brazil could become unsuitable by 2050, for example, while climate change will likely have "great impacts" on cocoa(可可粉) production in West Africa. "High quality chocolate will be less available in the future, and if you want it, you'll have to pay a lot more for it," Jarvis says.

在目前的条件下，世界上约有4%的农田在任何一年经历过干旱，但到本世纪末，这些条件预计将跃升至每年约18%。一些研究表明，园艺作物-通常，除了主食之外的所有作物-可能受到最严重的影响，主要是因为它们往往局限于较小的地理区域。例如，研究人员安德鲁·贾维斯（ Andrew Jarvis ）及其同事发现，到2050年，中美洲和巴西80%的咖啡种植区可能变得不合适，而气候变化可能会对西非的可可（可可可可粉）生产产生“巨大影响”。Jarvis说：「高品质的巧克力将来会减少，如果你想要它，你将不得不为此付出更多。」

H

This means that, for those who can afford it, some foods will simply cost them more in the future. But for poorer people, those same price jumps will likely cause certain foods to go extinct from their diets. "The more you reduce, the shorter the supply, and the higher the price will jump," Schlenker says.

这意味着，对于那些负担得起的人来说，一些食物在未来只会花费更多。但对于较贫穷的人来说，同样的价格上涨可能会导致某些食物从他们的饮食中消失。Schlenker说： “减少得越多，供应量就越短，价格就越高。”

I

Another potential climate change-induced problem is our dependence on commodity crops—wheat, soybeans, corn and rice—which currently provide humanity with 75% of its calories, either directly or indirectly through the animals we raise on those crops. Jarvis and his colleagues also found that, over the past five decades, the world has seen an increasing standardisation of diets; the foods we eat globally today are 36% more similar than they were in 1961. While this can be good news for the world's poorest people who now consume more calories, protein and fat than in the past, homogeneity(同一性) and over-dependence on a handful of staples leave us vulnerable to threats such as drought, disease and pests—all of which are predicted to worsen in many parts of the world as a result of climate change.

另一个潜在的气候变化引发的问题是我们对商品作物（小麦、大豆、玉米和大米）的依赖，这些作物目前直接或间接地通过我们在这些作物上饲养的动物为人类提供75%的卡路里。贾维斯和他的同事们还发现，在过去的五十年里，世界饮食的标准化程度越来越高；我们今天在全球范围内吃的食物比1961年多了36%。虽然这对世界上最贫穷的人来说可能是个好消息，他们现在比过去消耗更多的卡路里、蛋白质和脂肪，但同质性（同一性 ）和过度依赖少数几种主食使我们容易受到干旱、疾病和害虫等威胁--据预测，由于气候变化，世界许多地区都会恶化。

J

There are ways we could soften the coming blow to the global food supply, however. Like Farrant's work with resurrection crops, a number of companies, organisations and researchers are aiming to create drought and temperature-resistant crops through genetic engineering and conventional breeding. For now, the jury is still out as to how successful those endeavours will be. "The people at Monsanto who I've talked to are much more optimistic that they'll be able to engineer heat-tolerant crops," Schlenker says. "On the other hand, scientists at the USDA who I've spoken with are much more cautious."

然而，我们有一些方法可以缓解即将到来的对全球粮食供应的打击。与Farrant在复苏作物方面的工作一样，许多公司、组织和研究人员的目标是通过基因工程和传统育种创造耐旱耐温的作物。目前，陪审团仍不清楚这些努力的成功程度。Schlenker说： “与我交谈过的孟山都公司的人们更乐观地认为，他们将能够设计出耐热作物。”“另一方面，与我交谈过的美国农业部的科学家要谨慎得多。”

K

Until genetic engineering comes to fruition, other strategies might also help in some places, including applying more fertiliser, implementing better irrigation, using machinery that gets crops out of the field faster or installing storage facilities to delay spoilage. "Many places could benefit a great deal just by using technologies that already exist," Walsh says. "General farm management can go a long way toward easing changes."

在基因工程取得成果之前，其他策略也可能在某些地方有所帮助，包括施用更多肥料、实施更好的灌溉、使用更快地将作物从田间运走的机器或安装存储设施以延缓腐败变质。沃尔什说： “仅仅通过使用已经存在的技术，许多地方就可以受益匪浅。”“一般的农场管理可以大大缓解变化。”

L

Finally, diversifying our diet away from heat-sensitive wheat, corn, rice and other crops could also help. "We've seen profound changes in the last decades in what we eat largely as a result of international trade, and I think that trend toward more diversification will continue," M Jarvis says. "Depending on a greater number of plant species creates a more vigorous and less risky food system—and one that provides a broader range of nutritional requirements."

最后，将我们的饮食多样化，远离热敏性小麦、玉米、水稻和其他作物也可能有所帮助。“在过去的几十年里，由于国际贸易，我们的饮食发生了深刻的变化，我认为更多样化的趋势将继续下去，” M Jarvis说。“依赖于更多的植物物种，创造了一个更有活力、风险更低的食物系统，并提供了更广泛的营养需求。”
