新闻一。费耶特维尔市正特意表彰公交司机，迎接新设立的「公交司机感谢日」——该纪念日将于周六到来。
News Report 1 The city of Fayetteville is making a special effort to recognize its bus drivers ahead of the newly proclaimed Transit Driver Appreciation Day, happening Saturday.
作为活动的一部分，该市为在岗时间最长的司机庆祝。
As part of the event, the city is celebrating its longest-serving driver on the force.
这位司机是谢里·克里斯蒂安。
That driver is Sherry Christian.
克里斯蒂安已经当了 25 年司机。
Christian has been a driver for 25 years.
这位费耶特维尔本地人认为，开校车让她得以回馈社区。
The Fayetteville native believes driving a school bus allows her to give back to her community.
她说，这份工作最美好的部分，是看着坐她车的孩子们长大。
She says that the best part of the job is watching the children who ride her bus grow up.
早在 2009 年，「公交司机感谢日」就在全美各地庆祝了。
Transit Driver Appreciation Day has been celebrated across the country as far back as 2009.
不过本周，费耶特维尔市发布公告，把这一纪念日定在 3 月 18 日。
However, this week, the city of Fayetteville issued a proclamation for the day to take place March 18.
官员们说，他们想让公交司机知道自己对费耶特维尔有多重要——他们每年服务近 160 万人次乘客。
Officials said they want bus drivers to know how important they are to Fayetteville, serving almost 1.6 million riders a year.
新闻二。威尔顿教育委员会提议将支出增加 5.99%。
News Report 2 The Wilton School Board has proposed a 5.99% spending increase.
若获通过，这将是至少八个预算周期以来最大的增幅。
If approved, it would be the largest jump in at least eight budget cycles.
这一 5.99% 的请求，是过去八年同比增幅最高值（2021—2022 学年的 2.99%）的两倍多。
The 5.99% request more than doubles the highest previous year-over-year increase in the last eight years, which was 2.99 % for the 2021-2022 school year.
在这份 9180 万美元的拟议预算中，最大的支出项仍是教师工资。
The biggest driver of the $91.8 million proposed budget remains teachers' salaries.
这部分占总额的 64%，达 5900 万美元。
These account for 64% of the total request at $59 million.
这 5900 万美元高于本学年预算的 5670 万美元。
The $59 million is up from the $56 .7 million budgeted for the current school year.
有一些因素可能影响接下来的预算。
There are some factors that could affect the budget moving forward.
其中之一是总入学人数。
One of those is total enrolment.
该学区的总入学人数将为 3742 名学生。
The district will have a total enrolment of 3,742 students.
这些学生几乎都就读于幼儿园到高中阶段，但其中 66 人将就读学前班及其他项目。
Almost all of these students will be in kindergarten through high school, but 66 will be in pre-kindergarten and other programs.
这比本学年少了 47 名学生。
This represents 47 fewer students than the current year.
学生减少，意味着联邦和州政府的拨款也会减少。
Fewer students mean less federal and state funding.
新闻三。
News report 3.
苏格兰出现罕见的「雷雪」现象，受惊的当地居民以为听到了炸弹爆炸，纷纷报警。
A rare thundersnow event has taken place in Scotland, and alarmed locals contacted police, thinking they had heard bombs going off.
这种现象发生在雷电与强暴风雪交加之时，有时会发出巨响，容易被误认为爆炸。
The phenomenon happens when thunder and lightning mix with a heavy snowstorm, sometimes causing loud claps that can be confused with explosions.
有人形容那是一声巨响，像是飞机坠毁。
One person described it as a loud boom, similar to a plane crash.
另一个人在半夜被吵醒，他以为是施工队提前开工了。
Another person was woken up in the middle of the night by what he thought was early construction work starting.
声响之大足以震得楼房晃动、触发汽车警报。
The noise was powerful enough to shake buildings and set off car alarms.
警方接到大量市民来电，随即发布消息请民众保持冷静。
The police were flooded with many calls from the public, and they sent out a message asking people to remain calm.
警方让大家放心，这只是雷电，并非什么军事演习。
The police assured everyone that it was just thunder and lightning rather than some military exercise.
当时天气是当季最冷，因此提醒人们外出时注意安全。
The weather was the coldest it had been for the season, so people were warned to be careful when traveling outside.
尤其是路面结冰、地面湿滑等危险状况，会给司机带来困难。
In particular, dangerous conditions such as icy roads or wet ground could cause difficulty for drivers.
铁路部门也宣布，因积雪深厚，列车可能受到影响。
The rail service also announced potential disruptions to trains due to the deep snow.
一些道路也因恶劣天气而封闭。
Some roads were also closed because of the severe weather.
