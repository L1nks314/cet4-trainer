而不是来自自己同一种族的观察者。
as opposed to an observer from their own race.
与来自同一所大学的人相比，当他们与来自不同大学的人在一起时，情况也是如此。
The same was true when they were with someone from a different university compared to someone from their own.
研究人员表示，这是因为参与者预料自己会受到外来群体更多的负面评价。
Researchers say this was because participants anticipated more negative judgement from an outsider group.
相反，他们觉得自己群体内的成员对他们的评判更少。
In contrast, they felt less judged by members of their own group.
在一项实验中，参与者可以在糖果和水果之间选择一样作为零食。
In one experiment, participants were offered the choice between candy and fruit as a snack.
当在场的是本校一名不认识的学生时，只有 12% 的学生选择了更健康的那项。
When in the presence of an unknown student from their own university, only 12% of students selected the healthier option.
然而，当在场的是另一所大学一名不认识的学生时，这个数字变成了 31%。
However, this number was 31% when in the presence of an unknown student from another university.
其他实验按种族群体划分也显示出类似的结果。
Other experiments showed similar results based on racial group.
研究发现，人们感觉自己被其他群体的成员评判得更多。
The study found that people feel judged to a larger extent by members of other groups.
正因为如此，他们会策略性地选择健康食品，以给人留下好印象。
Because of this, they strategically use healthy food choices to make a positive impression.
人们做过许多尝试来帮助消费者做出更健康的选择，但消费者往往难以坚持健康饮食。
There have been many attempts to help consumers make healthier choices, but consumers often struggle to maintain a healthy diet.
这项研究发现，推广健康饮食的一种方法，也许是宣传健康选择带来的社交益处。
This research finds that one way to promote a healthy diet could be to advertise the social benefits of healthy choices.
短文二。
Passage 2.
根据最近一项研究的发现，对压力之下的高校学生来说，狗也许是最好的抗压帮手。
For college students under pressure, a dog may be the best stress fighter around according to the finding of a recent study.
这是一个非常有说服力的发现，该研究的负责人帕特里夏·彭德里说。
It's a really powerful finding, said Patricia Pendry, who led the study.
高校在帮助学生学业成功方面做了很多出色的工作，尤其是对那些因有心理健康问题病史或学业和学习问题而可能陷入困境的学生。
Universities are doing a lot of great work trying to help students succeed academically, especially those who may be at risk due to a history of mental health issues or academic and learning issues.
这项研究表明，对这一群体而言，传统的压力管理方法不如那些着重提供与治疗犬互动机会的项目有效。
This study shows that traditional stress management approaches aren't as effective for this population compared with programs that focus on providing opportunities to interact with therapy dogs.
研究人员测量了参与这项研究学生的执行功能。
The researchers measured executive functioning in the students involved in the study.
执行功能是指一个人进行计划、组织、激励、集中注意力和记忆所需的各种技能。
Executive function is a term for the skills one needs to plan, organise, motivate, concentrate and memorise.
这些都是学生要在大学取得好成绩所需的技能。
These are skills students need to succeed in college.
研究人员发现，风险最高的学生在与治疗犬互动之后，执行功能改善得最多。
The researchers found that students who were most at risk had the most improvements in executive functioning after interacting with therapy dogs.
六周后研究人员进行随访时，这些结果依然成立。
These results remained when researchers followed up six weeks later.
许多高校都提供学业压力管理项目和研讨班。
Many universities provide academic stress management programs and workshops.
这些项目历来与大学课堂非常相似。
These are traditionally very similar to college classes.
它们常常讲的是如何多睡觉、设定目标，或者管理压力和焦虑。
They often talk about ways to get more sleep, set goals or manage stress or anxiety.
彭德里承认，这些都是非常重要的主题，这些研讨班能帮助普通学生取得成功，但对那些学得吃力的学生帮助较小。
Pendry acknowledges that these are really important topics and these workshops help typical students succeed, but they're less helpful for struggling students.
彭德里认为，这些学生可能会把这些项目当成又一场讲座，反而感到压力更大。
Pendry believes those students may experience the programs as another lecture and feel even more stressed.
短文三。
Passage 3.
在商场上冒险并不意味着盲目地投身商海，然后指望获得巨大成功。
Taking risks in business does not mean going into business blindly and then expecting great results.
恰恰相反，创业中的冒险需要周密的计划和辛勤的努力。
On the contrary, taking risks in entrepreneurship involves careful planning and hard work.
无论冒险前经过多么精心的算计，没有人能真正确定冒险是否会带来成功。
Nobody can really be sure if risks will be met with success, no matter how calculated they may be.
但这不应阻止你去冒险，因为如果你想让事业成功，冒险是必不可少的。
But this should not stop you from taking risks, as risks are necessary if you want your business to succeed.
有些冒险可能不会成功，但乐观的冒险者总会把失败看作学习的机会。
Some risks may not work out, but an optimistic risk taker will always look at failure as an opportunity to learn.
愿意尝试新想法是事业成长的关键。
The willingness to experiment with new ideas is key to business growth.
正如老话所说，不入虎穴，焉得虎子。
As the old saying goes, nothing ventured, nothing gained.
失败会教你如何有策略地思考和规划。
Failure will teach you how to think and plan strategically.
但要记住，并非所有冒险都是好的冒险，当你失败时，要从中吸取教训，继续前行。
But just remember that not all risks are good ones, and when you fail, learn from it and move forward.
由于大多数人倾向于规避风险，那些勇于冒险的企业就已经拥有了竞争优势。
Since most people tend to avoid risk, businesses that are brave enough to take risks already have a competitive advantage.
正是这些企业以新点子、新方案和大胆的发明树立着行业标准。
They are the ones setting the standard with new ideas, fresh offers, and bold inventions.
冒险者在困难时期最善于应变。
Risk takers are best at adapting in difficult times.
简而言之，当大多数人远离风险时，就意味着冒险者面临的竞争更少。
Simply put, when most individuals stay away from risk, it means less competition for risk takers.
我们不知道你是否能取得这些冒险者那样的成就，但只要你一直想求稳，只要你满足于事业目前的现状，你就永远不会知道答案。
We don't know if you'll achieve what these risk takers have achieved, but for as long as you want to stay safe, and for as long as you are content with where your business is right now, you will never find out.
