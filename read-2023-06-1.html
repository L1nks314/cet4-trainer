第 1 篇 · 第 46–50 题

5 段 · 5 题
P1

The United States is facing a housing crisis: Affordable housing is inadequate, while luxury homes abound(充裕), and homelessness remains a persistent problem. Despite this, popular culture and the housing industry market happiness as living with both more space and more amenities(便利设施). Big houses are advertised as a reward for hard work and diligence, turning housing from a basic necessity into a luxury.

P2

This is reflected in our homes. The average single-family home built in the United States before 1970 was less than 1,500 square feet in size. By 2016, the average size of a new, single-family home was 2,422 square feet. What's more, homes built in the 2000s were more likely than earlier models to have more of all types of spaces: bedrooms, bathrooms, living rooms, dining rooms, recreation rooms and garages.

P3

There are consequences of living big. As middle-class houses have grown larger, two things have happened. First, large houses take time to maintain, so cleaners and other low-wage service workers are required to keep these houses in order. Second, once-public spaces, where people from diverse backgrounds used to come together, have increasingly become privatized, leading to a reduction in the number of public facilities available to all, and a reduced quality of life for many. Take swimming pools. While in 1950, only 2,500 U.S. families owned pools, by 1999 this number was 4 million. At the same time, public municipal pools were often closed, leaving low-income people nowhere to swim.

P4

The trend for bigger housing thus poses ethical questions. Should Americans accept a system in which the middle and upper classes enjoy a luxurious lifestyle, using the low-wage labor of others? Are we willing to accept a system in which an increase in amenities purchased by the affluent means a reduction in amenities for the poor?

P5

I believe neither is acceptable. We must change the way we think: living well does not need to mean having more private spaces; instead, it could mean having more public spaces. A better goal than building bigger houses for some is to create more publicly accessible spaces and amenities for all.

46.答案 B细节题展开解析
判型

Q46 问的是 What are big houses promoted to be，要的是文中明写的一个事实陈述，不需推理。

拆句

Q46 是一个被动语态疑问句，主干为"big houses are promoted to be 什么"，promoted to be 后面接的内容就是答案要补的成分，结构平铺，无陷阱。

定位
P1

Big houses are advertised as a reward for hard work and diligence, turning housing from a basic necessity into a luxury.

大房子被宣传成对努力工作和勤勉的一种回报，把住房从基本必需品变成了奢侈品。

选项
A张冠李戴

原文宣传是面向勤奋者的回报，A luxury for the homeless 把奢侈品安到 homeless 头上，而原文 homelessness 只是危机背景，并非大房子的宣传对象。

B✓ 正确
a reward for hard work and diligence↔A reward for industriousness

预判的"对勤奋的回报"在 A reward for industriousness 中 reward 与 industriousness 双双对应，锁定该项。

C答非所问

An abundant source of comforts 取自第三段及背景里 amenities/abound 的语义，但 Q46 问的是大房子被"宣传成什么"，定位句给的是 reward，不是"舒适之源"。

D与原文相反

原文恰恰说大房子使住房 from a basic necessity into a luxury（从必需变奢侈），An absolute necessity for happiness 反把它说成"幸福的绝对必需品"，与原文相反。

47.答案 A细节题展开解析
判型

What is one of the consequences 用 one of 限定，答案是原文列举的多项后果中的一项，属可直接定位的细节。

拆句

Q47 主干为"What is one of the consequences"，of living big 是后置定语修饰 consequences，one of 提示原文有多项后果，结构简单。

定位
P3

Second, once-public spaces, where people from diverse backgrounds used to come together, have increasingly become privatized, leading to a reduction in the number of public facilities available to all, and a reduced quality of life for many.

第二，曾经的公共空间——不同背景的人过去常在此相聚——日益被私有化，导致面向所有人的公共设施数量减少，许多人的生活质量随之下降。

选项
A✓ 正确
a reduced quality of life for many↔Many Americans' quality of life has become lower

预判的"生活质量下降"与 quality of life has become lower 字面同义，直接锁定。

B过度推断

原文只说公共空间被私有化、不同背景的人"过去"常相聚（used to），并未说人们 no longer socialize（彻底不再社交），属过度引申。

C以偏概全

游泳池只是说明私有化的一个例子，原文说 public municipal pools were often closed（常被关闭），no longer have access to public swimming pools 把"常被关"绝对成"再也用不上"，且以个例顶替本题要的总后果。

D偷换概念

原文受影响的是 quality of life（生活质量），private life has been negatively affected 偷换成 private life（私人生活），概念不同。

48.答案 A细节题展开解析
判型

What questions arise from living big 问的是原文明确点出的问题性质，文中有直接对应的形容词限定，属细节。

拆句

Q48 主干"What questions arise"，from living big 为原因状语，整句问"住大房子引发哪类问题"，无复杂从句。

定位
P4

The trend for bigger housing thus poses ethical questions.

因此，住房越来越大的趋势引出了伦理（道德）层面的问题。

选项
A✓ 正确
ethical questions↔Questions related to moral principles

预判的"伦理/道德问题"与 moral principles 同义，直接锁定。

B答非所问

labor cost 取自第四段反问里 using the low-wage labor of others，但那只是伦理问题的内容举例，问题的"性质"是伦理性，labor cost 答非所问。

C张冠李戴

lifestyle to promote 接近第五段"该提倡什么生活方式"的话题，但 Q48 问的是第四段引发的问题性质，what lifestyle to promote 张冠李戴到了下一段内容。

D无中生有

housing development（住房开发/发展）在第四段问题里并无对应表述，原文谈的是伦理而非开发问题，属凭空捏造。

49.答案 D细节题展开解析
判型

What kind of social system does the author think is unacceptable 答案在原文有 a system in which... 的直接描述，是可定位的细节，而非需推断的整体态度。

拆句

Q49 主干"What kind of social system is unacceptable"，中间插入 does the author think 表明这是作者的判断，从句嵌套但语义清晰：找作者认为不可接受的那种制度。

定位
P4

Are we willing to accept a system in which an increase in amenities purchased by the affluent means a reduction in amenities for the poor?

我们是否愿意接受这样一种制度：富人购买的便利设施增加，就意味着穷人可享的便利设施减少？

选项
A以偏概全

原文第一个反问确提到 using the low-wage labor of others，但 the low-wage laborers building their houses 把"他人的低薪劳动"窄化为"盖房工人"，且第二个反问（富人便利↔穷人便利）才是更完整的对应，A 项以偏概全。

B无中生有

原文说富人购买便利使穷人便利减少，从未谈 an increasingly unjustifiable price（越来越不合理的价格），价格维度是捏造的。

C偷换概念

deprive the lower classes of affordable housing（剥夺下层的平价住房）取自首段背景 Affordable housing is inadequate，但作者第四段否定的制度是"便利的增减对立"，不是剥夺平价住房，概念被偷换。

D✓ 正确
an increase in amenities purchased by the affluent means a reduction in amenities for the poor↔the affluent enjoy a more comfortable life at the expense of the poor

预判的"富人享乐、穷人受损"恰是 enjoy a more comfortable life at the expense of the poor 的含义，锁定该项。

50.答案 C细节题展开解析
判型

What does the author advocate 答案是末段作者直接给出的建议，有明写对应句，属细节定位题。

拆句

Q50 主干"What does the author advocate"，for people to live well 是目的状语（为了让人们住得好），整句问"作者为此提倡什么"，结构清楚。

定位
P5

A better goal than building bigger houses for some is to create more publicly accessible spaces and amenities for all.

比起为少数人盖更大的房子，更好的目标是为所有人创造更多可公共使用的空间与设施。

选项
A无中生有

作者主张是"创造更多公共空间"，并未说要把私人空间 turn private spaces into public ones（改造成公共空间），这一具体做法原文没有。

B与原文相反

Building more houses affordable 回到了"盖房子"，而作者主张的恰恰是别再盖大房子、转向公共空间，且 affordable housing 是首段背景非作者的提倡，方向与主张相悖。

C✓ 正确
create more publicly accessible spaces and amenities for all↔More public spaces created for everyone to enjoy

预判的"为人人创造公共空间"与 More public spaces created for everyone to enjoy 字面同义，锁定该项。

D偷换概念

原文是"为所有人创造公共空间与设施"，All amenities made accessible to the rich and the poor alike 用 All amenities 把范围绝对化为"所有便利设施都对贫富开放"，超出作者所说的"公共空间"范围。

第 2 篇 · 第 51–55 题

8 段 · 5 题
P1

Most of us in the entrepreneurial community are blessed—or cursed—with higher-than-average ambition. Ambitious people strongly desire accomplishments and are willing to take more risks and spend more effort to get them.

P2

Overall, this is a positive quality, especially for people trying to build their own businesses. Apparently, if you're more naturally driven to set goals, you are more likely to succeed.

P3

Actually, this isn't always the case. In fact, in some cases, extreme ambition may end up doing more harm than good.

P4

One major side effect of excessive ambition is the tendency to focus too determinedly on one particular vision or end goal. This is problematic because it hinders your ability to adapt to new circumstances, which is vital if you want to be a successful entrepreneur. If a new competitor emerges to threaten your business, you may need to change direction, even if that means straying from your original vision. If you have too much ambition, you'll find this hard, if not impossible.

P5

Few people are successful when they try to build their first brand. Unfortunately, for the most ambitious entrepreneurs, a failure is seen as disastrous, and impossible to recover from.

P6

It's a clear departure from the intended plan toward the intended goal. For people with limited ambition, however, failure is viewed as something closer to reality. Remember, failure is inevitable, and every failure you survive is a learning experience.

P7

Ambitious people tend to be more materialistically successful than their non-ambitious counterparts. However, they're only slightly happier than their less-ambitious counterparts, and tend to live significantly shorter lives. This implies that even though ambitious people are more likely to achieve conventional "success," such success means nothing for their health and happiness—and if you don't have health and happiness, what else could possibly matter?

P8

Clearly, some amount of ambition is good for your motivation. Without any ambition, you wouldn't start your own business, set or achieve goals and get far in life. But an excess of ambition can also be dangerous, putting you at risk of burnout, stubbornness and even a shorter life.

51.答案 B细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What does the author think of most entrepreneurs? 直接问对一个明确对象的看法，答案能在原文某句找到对应陈述，不需跨段综合，属细节题而非推断。

拆句

题干 What does the author think of most entrepreneurs? 是 think of 引导的特殊疑问句，think of sb 意为"对某人的看法"，结构平铺直叙、无陷阱。

定位
P1

Most of us in the entrepreneurial community are blessed—or cursed—with higher-than-average ambition.

我们创业圈里的大多数人，都被赋予——或者说被困于——高于常人的野心。

选项
A偷换概念

原文说野心者 willing to take more risks 指愿冒事业风险，选项却说 risk their own lives（拿命冒险），把"风险"偷换成"生命危险"。

B✓ 正确
higher-than-average ambition↔more ambitious than ordinary people

选项 They are more ambitious than ordinary people 与预判"野心高于普通人"完全吻合，是定位句的同义替换。

C无中生有

原文谈的是 conventional "success"（传统成功），选项却说 nonconventional success（非传统成功），文中从无此说法。

D以偏概全

原文只说野心 this is a positive quality 是"一种"积极特质，选项却说创业者 more positive qualities than most of us"比我们多"，既夸大数量又把比较对象搞错（原文 most of us 本就包含创业者）。

52.答案 A词义句意题展开解析
判型

题干 What does the author imply by saying "this isn't always the case" 用 imply by saying 问一个具体句子的含义，要求理解 this 指代什么、否定的是什么，属词义句意题。

拆句

题干主干 What does the author imply，by saying "..." 是方式状语；关键在引文里的 this 是指代词，须回原文找它替代的上文内容；isn't always 是部分否定，意为"并非总是"，不是全盘否定。

定位
P3

Actually, this isn't always the case.

实际上，情况并非总是如此。

选项
A✓ 正确
you are more likely to succeed↔may not have a greater chance of success

选项 Ambitious people may not have a greater chance of success 正是"this（野心者更易成功）＋部分否定"的还原，与预判一致。

B偷换概念

this 指代的是"更易成功"，并非"积极特质"；选项 may not have more positive qualities 把否定矛头指向上一段的 positive quality，找错了 this 的指代。

C与原文相反

原文只说极端野心 may end up doing more harm than good（弊"大于"利），选项却说 does as much good as harm（利害"相当"），把"大于"篡改成"相等"。

D与原文相反

选项 more naturally driven to success 几乎照搬上文 more naturally driven to set goals，可这恰是本句要否定的观点，方向正好相反。

53.答案 B细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What does the author say is of extreme importance 用 say 直问作者明说的内容，答案是原文一句强调重要性的话，属细节题。

拆句

题干 What does the author say is of extreme importance for one to become a successful entrepreneur?，主干是 What...is of extreme importance；of+抽象名词等于形容词，of extreme importance＝extremely important；for one to become... 是不定式目的状语，指明"为了成为成功创业者"。

定位
P4

This is problematic because it hinders your ability to adapt to new circumstances, which is vital if you want to be a successful entrepreneur.

这是有问题的，因为它会损害你适应新环境的能力，而这种能力对于想成为成功创业者的人至关重要。

选项
A与原文相反

原文说过度野心导致 focus too determinedly on one particular vision 反而有害，需 straying from your original vision；选项 Holding on to one's original vision（死守原愿景）正是文章要批评的做法。

B✓ 正确
adapt to new circumstances↔adapt to new situations

选项 Being able to adapt to new situations 与预判"适应新环境的能力"几乎逐词对应，确为答案。

C与原文相反

选项 Focusing determinedly on one particular goal 直接抄自 focus too determinedly on one particular vision，而原文说这是过度野心的"副作用"，绝非"极其重要"之事。

D与原文相反

原文说有时须 change direction"改变方向"，选项却说 Avoiding radical change in one's career direction"避免大改方向"，与文意相左。

54.答案 C细节题展开解析
判型

题干 How do the most ambitious entrepreneurs regard failure 问一个明确群体对失败的态度，原文有明文表述，属细节题。

拆句

题干 How do the most ambitious entrepreneurs regard failure in their endeavor?，regard sth 意为"看待某事"，in their endeavor（在其奋斗中）是范围状语；结构清晰、无陷阱。

定位
P5

Unfortunately, for the most ambitious entrepreneurs, a failure is seen as disastrous, and impossible to recover from.

不幸的是，对那些野心最大的创业者来说，一次失败被视为灾难性的、且无法翻身的。

选项
A张冠李戴

awaken them to reality 对应的是第六段 failure is viewed as something closer to reality，那讲的是"野心有限者"，而非题干问的"野心最大者"。

B张冠李戴

a lesson they have to learn 对应第六段 every failure you survive is a learning experience，同样是说"野心有限者"，张冠李戴。

C✓ 正确
disastrous, and impossible to recover from↔means the end of their career

选项 It means the end of their career 把"灾难、无法恢复"提炼成"事业到头"，与预判一致，确为答案。

D与原文相反

原文说 impossible to recover from"无法恢复"，选项却说 result in a slow recovery"导致缓慢恢复"，恢复从"不可能"被偷改成"慢"。

55.答案 D推断题展开解析
判型

题干 What does the author advise us to do concerning ambition? 问作者的建议，原文未必有一句直说"我建议你……"，需综合全文对野心利弊的论述提炼，属推断题。

拆句

题干 What does the author advise us to do concerning ambition?，advise sb to do 意为"建议某人做某事"，concerning ambition（关于野心）是话题状语；结构平铺直叙、无陷阱。

选项
A偷换概念

原文要区分的是 conventional "success" 与 health and happiness，选项 Distinguish between conventional success and our life goal 把后一项换成了文中没有的"人生目标"，区分对象被改错。

B与原文相反

全文都在指出 the most ambitious entrepreneurs 看待失败的方式有害，选项 Follow the example of the most ambitious entrepreneurs 却劝人效仿他们，与作者立场正相反。

C以偏概全

Avoid taking unnecessary risks 只关乎 take more risks 这一点，并非全文劝诫的落脚；作者最终落点是健康幸福，而非"少冒险"，以偏概全且无中生有。

D✓ 正确
such success means nothing for their health and happiness—and if you don't have health and happiness, what else could possibly matter?↔Prioritize health and happiness over material success

选项 Prioritize health and happiness over material success 与主线"健康幸福重于成功"吻合，且统摄全文对过度野心的批评，是建议题应有的全局答案。
