短文二。
Passage 2.
讲故事是领导者用来影响、教导和激励他人最有力的手段之一。
Telling stories is one of the most powerful means leaders have to influence, teach, and inspire.
讲故事能在人与人之间建立联系。
Storytelling forges connections among people.
谈到我们的国家、我们的社区和我们的家庭时，我们本能地明白，我们共同拥有的故事是维系纽带的重要部分。

这种理解在商业世界同样成立，在那里，一个组织的故事以及其领导者所讲的故事，能以事实陈述做不到的方式巩固人际关系。
This understanding also holds true in the business world, where an organization's stories, and the stories its leaders tell, help solidify relationships in a way that factual statements don't.
好故事所做的不仅仅是营造一种联系感。
Good stories do more than create a sense of connection.
它们能建立熟悉感和信任。

它们让听者进入故事之中，使他们更愿意学习。

他们能够以一种没有风险的方式去处理各种情形。
They're able to work through situations in a way that's risk-free.
对领导者来说还有额外的好处。
There are extra benefits for leaders.
借助一个简单、个人的故事，他们可以传达根本的价值观。
With a simple, personal story, they can convey fundamental values.
他们可以让人洞见其自身经验和知识的演变过程，从而显得更平易近人。
They can offer insight into the evolution of their own experience and knowledge, presenting themselves as more approachable.
这样一来，他们就更有可能激励他人想要了解更多。
And then they are more likely to inspire others to want to know more.

而这一比例在总人口中仅为19%。
This can be compared to just 19% of the general population.

The values and habits they bring from their own homelands help explain some of the success.
发展中国家的失业率往往更高。
Unemployment tends to be higher in developing countries.
这意味着更多的人必须去创业。
