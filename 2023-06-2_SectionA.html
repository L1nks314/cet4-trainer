那条蛇没有毒，所以这名男子不必去医院，但他的手臂在现场接受了医疗处理。
The snake was not poisonous, so the man did not have to go to the hospital, but his arm was medically treated at the scene.
接受采访的警官告诉新闻记者，那条蛇并不属于这名男子，目前尚不清楚它是如何进入公寓的。
The police officer interviewed, told news reporters the snake didn't belong to the man, and it was unclear how it got into the apartment.
那条蛇最终被当地的动物处理人员捕获，随后被送往一家动物医院。
The snake was eventually captured by the local animal handlers and taken to an animal hospital afterward.

新闻二。
News report 2.
自去年4月以来，安德里亚·贝尔彻每周倒垃圾时都会做一件略有些不同寻常又有趣的事。
Every week since last April, Andrea Belcher has been doing something slightly unusual and amusing when she takes out her trash.
这一切始于一位电影明星分享了一张自己穿着舞会礼服把垃圾桶搬出去的照片。
It all started off when a movie star shared a photo of herself putting her trash bins out in a ball gown.
这激励安德里亚打扮一番后再去倒垃圾。
This inspired Andrea to get dressed up to take out the trash.
她不好意思在白天这么做，于是在一个漆黑的夜晚，她穿上那件黑色露肩礼服，把垃圾桶搬了出去。
She was too embarrassed to do it during the day, so one dark evening, she put on her black off-the-shoulder gown and put her trash bin out.
她的女儿把这一幕拍了下来，全家人都笑了，但这件事似乎颇受欢迎。
Her daughter filmed it, and the whole family laughed, but it seemed to go down well.
接下来的几周，她在自己的装扮箱里又找到别的服装，扮成不同的角色和名人，并在社交媒体上分享自己的这一趣事。
The following weeks, she found other outfits in her dressing-up box and dressed up as different characters and famous people, sharing her exploits on social media.
没过多久，人们开始主动拿出自己的漂亮裙子给她穿，不知不觉间，她已经坚持了20周。
Before long, people started offering up their own fancy dresses for her to wear, and before she knew it, she had done 20 weeks.
在解释自己为何一直拍摄这些视频时，安德里亚说，能有这么一点乐趣和疯狂、让人们露出笑容，感觉真好。
Explaining why she has kept up the videos, Andrea explained how nice it was to have a little bit of fun and craziness that made people smile.
此外，这也有助于提醒社区里的人们每周把垃圾桶搬出去。
Besides, it also helps to remind people in the community to put out their trash bins each week.

News Report 3.
没钱，但还是饿？
No money, but still hungry?
没问题。
No problem.
欢迎来到弗兰克餐厅，这里的顾客在付完餐费后把收据留在了一块板子上。
Welcome to Frank's Restaurant, where customers are paying for meals and leaving receipts on a board.
任何饥饿、想吃点东西的人都可以拿走一张，无需多问，然后把它交给店员换取食物。
Anyone who is hungry and wants something to eat can take one, no questions asked, and hand it to the staff for some food.
免费餐板这一想法始于一位捐赠者走进店里，为每位员工各给了100美元。
The idea for the board of free meals started after a donor came in with $100 for each member of its staff.
据餐厅老板塔拉·莫里斯说，在两名员工各自归还了属于他们的那份钱后，还剩下了700美元，于是他们决定做点更多的事，便设立了这块板子。
After two staffers returned their share, there was $700 left over, according to Tara Morris, the owner of the restaurant, so they decided to do something more by creating the board.
餐厅里的这块免费餐板显示了许多顾客已经预付的潜在餐食，每份大约10美元。
The board of free meals inside the restaurant shows a host of potential meals already paid for by customers, each costing about $10.
板子上写着善意的话语，比如“如果你想让世界上有更多善意，就把它留在这里”。
The board has messages of kindness, like, if you want more kindness in the world, put it here.
这项活动已经持续了两周，莫里斯说，有不到50位顾客领到了一份免费餐。
The drive has been going on for two weeks, and Morris said just under 50 customers have picked up a free meal.
目前没有关于有人趁机占便宜的报道。
There have been no reports of anyone taking advantage of the situation.
事实上，给餐券的人比领餐券的人多得多。
In fact, there are a lot more people giving tickets than receiving.
看来人们想要帮忙，并且被帮助他人所驱动。
It seems people want to help and are driven by helping others.
