参考范文（169 词）

范文 + 参考译文

While many college students bury themselves in books and treat workouts as a waste of time, it is often overlooked that physical exercise plays an indispensable role in achieving success at college. In my view, staying active is not the enemy of study but its solid foundation.

First, regular exercise strengthens the body and guarantees the energy that intense coursework demands. A student who jogs every morning is more likely to stay focused through back-to-back lectures than one who never moves. Moreover, working out is a powerful way to relieve stress. When exams and job hunting pile up, a quick run on the track can wash away tension and rebuild mental resilience. In addition, sticking to a fixed exercise routine cultivates self-discipline, and this habit naturally transfers to managing study plans on time.

In short, physical exercise is by no means a distraction; rather, it is the cornerstone of college success. Therefore, every student is advised to fit regular workouts into daily life before body and mind break down.

第1段
现象引入加亮立场 本段先用 While many college students bury themselves in books 引出忽视锻炼的普遍现象，再用 physical exercise plays an indispensable role in achieving success at college 斩钉截铁亮出立场，落实了第一段『亮明立场』的功能，且把题眼 role 直接写进首段。
第2段
三角度各带场景 本段用 First / Moreover / In addition 串起身体、心理、自律三个角度，每个角度都配了校园场景（晨跑保持专注、操场短跑驱散紧张、固定作息培养自律），落实了『主题句加理由加例子加小结』的论证方法，避免了只喊口号。
第3段
否定误解再呼吁 本段用 by no means a distraction; rather, it is the cornerstone 否定误解、重申立场，再用 every student is advised to fit regular workouts into daily life 发出行动呼吁，落实了结尾段『重申加呼吁』的功能。
自检避坑
典型低分写法 · 反面示例与改法
写成锻炼方法
反面示例『To stay healthy, you should run three times a week and eat more vegetables.』——这是在教人怎么锻炼，完全没碰 the role of physical exercise in achieving success at college 里的 role。
改法换成『Running regularly keeps students energetic enough to handle heavy coursework.』，把动作改成『对大学成功的作用』。
只喊口号无例证
反面示例『Exercise is very important. It is good for us. We should all do exercise because it helps a lot.』——三句都在重复『重要』，没有任何论证，且在注水凑字。
改法换成带场景的论证，如『A student who jogs every morning stays focused through back-to-back lectures.』
中式英语逐字直译
反面示例『Open exercise can let your body become more strong and study very high.』——『open exercise』『study very high』都是逐字硬翻，不成英文。
改法用地道搭配重写，如『Regular exercise builds a stronger body and boosts academic performance.』

任务
核心任务论锻炼对大学成功的作用 一句话钉死：本篇要论证体育锻炼在大学取得成功中扮演的角色，即 the role of physical exercise in achieving success at college。重心是『作用』，不是『锻炼方法』也不是『我爱的运动』。
立意
中心立意锻炼是大学成功的基石 中心论点：体育锻炼并非课业的对立面，而是支撑大学成功的基石。它直接回应 the role of physical exercise in achieving success at college 中的 role，亮明『不可或缺』的立场。
要点
要展开的要点强健体魄保障学习精力 锻炼增强体质、提升专注与续航力，让学生扛得住备考与连堂课，这是大学成功的体力底盘——直接服务于 achieving success at college。 运动缓解压力提升心理韧性 考试、社团、求职的多重压力下，规律运动能释放紧张、稳定情绪，使人保持清醒与抗挫力，是学业可持续的心理保障。 坚持锻炼磨炼自律品格 每日定时锻炼需要自我管理，这份自律与团队运动里的协作精神会迁移到学习中，转化为按计划推进任务的能力，呼应题目所问的 role。
大纲
段落大纲第 1 段 亮明立场 开门见山指出体育锻炼对大学成功不可或缺，呼应 the role of physical exercise in achieving success at college，为全文定调。 第 2 段 分角度论证 从身体、心理、自律三个角度论证锻炼如何助力 achieving success at college，每个角度配一个具体校园场景。 第 3 段 重申并呼吁 重申锻炼是大学成功的基石，呼吁同学把锻炼纳入日常，收束全文。
