对话二。
Conversation 2.
今天《谈书》节目的嘉宾是约翰·布莱克，他是新晋畅销书《提前退休》的作者。
Our guest on today's Book Talk is John Black, the author of the new bestseller, Retire Early.
谢谢你邀请我上节目，莉莎。
Thanks for having me on the show, Lisa.
约翰，你的书引起了很大关注，部分原因是你在书中写道，大多数人都能在 40 岁退休。
John, your book is getting a lot of attention, partly because you write that most people can retire by 40.
这现实吗？
Is that realistic?
绝对现实。
Definitely.
为写这本书做调研时，我采访了数百位在 40 岁就退休的人。
When researching this book, I interviewed hundreds of people who retired by 40.
好吧，但一个人要退休需要多少钱呢？
OK, but how much money does a person need to retire?
我读过一些文章，建议 100 万美元是退休的理想数额。
I've read articles recommending $1 million as a good figure for retirement.
虽然大多数理财规划师确实会给出具体数字，但我认为这没什么用，因为人们生活在不同的地区，生活成本不同，需求和愿望也不同，所以我才建议大家以财务独立为目标。
While most financial planners do give numbers, I don't think that's useful, as people are living in different areas with different costs of living and have different needs and wants, which is why I suggest that people aim for financial independence.
就是投资收入大于每月的开支。
Having investment income that's greater than monthly expenses.
在我看来这听起来有风险。
That sounds risky to me.
人们不是应该留有应急的现金吗？
Shouldn't people have extra cash for emergencies?
有太多人因为害怕，一辈子都在做自己讨厌的工作，所以我劝人们去冒点险。
Too many people spend their lives working on jobs they hate because they're afraid, so I counsel people to take risks.
退休不必是永久的，如果人们需要，也可以重新回去工作。
Retirement doesn't have to be permanent, and if people need to, they can go back to work.
但离开职场很久之后，想重新回去工作并不总是那么容易。
But it's not always easy to get back into the workforce after an extended absence.
没错，但如果你能让自己的技能跟上时代，就会受到雇主的青睐。
True, but if you keep current with your skills, you will be attractive to employers.
也许吧，可大多数人在 60 岁都退不了休，又怎么可能有人在 40 岁前攒够钱退休呢？
Maybe, but how can anyone save enough to retire by 40 when most people can't retire at 60?
很简单。
Simple.
削减住房、食品和交通开支，并把月收入的一半用于投资。
By cutting housing, food and transport expenses and investing half your monthly income.
这听起来根本不可能。
That sounds impossible.
我承认这很难，但对成千上万人都奏效了。
I admit it's difficult, but it has worked for thousands.
