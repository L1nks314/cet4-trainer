新闻一。
News Report 1.
官员称，周六主街一栋建筑起火后，六人不得不离开家搬到别处。
Six people had to move away from their home to another place after a fire broke out in a building on Main Street Saturday, officials said.
据诺沃克市消防局副局长亚当·马科维茨称，下午 1 点刚过，消防员就赶赴这栋三层建筑，处理一起上报的建筑火灾。
Firefighters responded to the three-storey building shortly after 1 p.m. for a reported structure fire, according to Norwalk Deputy Fire Chief Adam Markowitz.
马科维茨说，消防队员到达时遇到二楼冒出浓烟。
Markowitz said crews encountered heavy smoke coming from the second floor when they arrived.
随后一支约 25 人的消防队花了约 25 分钟扑灭大火。
A team of about 25 firefighters then spent about 25 minutes extinguishing the flames.
官员将这栋建筑描述为混合用途建筑，一层是商业店铺，二、三层是住宅。
Officials described the structure as a mixed-use building that features commercial businesses on the first floor and residential on the second and third floors.
镇上的记录显示该建筑内有四套公寓。
Town records list four apartments in the building.
官员称，由于烟熏和高温损坏，这四套公寓被宣布不宜居住，六名住户不得不搬到别处。
Due to smoke and heat damage, the four apartments were declared uninhabitable and the six residents had to move to another place, officials said.
据报没有人在此次火灾中受伤。
No injuries were reported in connection with the fire.
诺沃克消防局长正在调查火灾的起因和起火点。
The Norwalk Fire Marshal is investigating the cause and origin of the fire.
新闻二。
News Report 2.
一项新研究对一些历史研究提出了质疑，这些研究认为一个人出生的季节或月份与某些心理健康问题风险增加有关。
A new study has cast doubt on historic research suggesting that the season or month of someone's birth is associated with an increased risk of certain mental health conditions.
该研究考察了欧洲 7 万多名老年人的焦虑和抑郁症状。
The study looks at symptoms of anxiety and depression among more than 70,000 older adults in Europe.
过去有不少研究发现了出生季节与心理健康诊断之间的联系。
A number of past studies have found the link between season of birth and mental health diagnoses.
研究人员提出，这种联系可能源于各种因素。
Researchers have suggested that such links could arise from various things.
这些因素包括一年中随季节变化的营养摄入、日照、气候和疾病暴露。
These include nutrient intake, sun exposure, climate and disease exposure varying across the course of the year.
然而，证据一直莫衷一是。
However, evidence has been mixed.
较新的研究认为，与出生月份相比，社会阶层或经济背景等因素与这些诊断的关系更大。
More recent studies have suggested that factors such as social class or economic background have more to do with these diagnoses than month of birth.
总体而言，这项新研究没有发现参与者的出生月份与抑郁或焦虑症状之间存在显著关系。
Overall, the new study found no significant relationship between participants' month of birth and symptoms of depression or anxiety.
在一些国家存在一定差异。
There was some variability in some countries.
在波兰，抑郁症状会因出生月份不同而略有波动。
In Poland, depressive symptoms fluctuated a little depending on birth month.
在捷克，焦虑症状也是如此。
In the Czech Republic, the same was true of anxiety symptoms.
但总体来看，并不存在系统性的规律。
But on the whole, there was no systematic pattern.
新闻三。
News report 3.
中国的基因研究人员克隆出一只明星警犬。
Genetic researchers in China have made a clone of a star police dog.
这只克隆犬在 12 月出生在北京的一家实验室。
The clone was born in a laboratory in Beijing in December.
检测表明，这只克隆犬和她的母犬在基因上几乎完全相同。
Tests show that the clone and her mother are almost identical genetically.
那只母犬曾帮助侦破多起谋杀案和许多其他案件。
The mother dog helped solve multiple murders and many other crimes.
这只克隆犬在多项测试中已经表现得比传统繁育的犬更好。
The clone has already performed better than traditionally bred dogs on several tests.
如果这只克隆犬继续达到预期表现，这可能意味着警犬训练时间的大幅缩短，而警犬训练通常需要约五年。
If the clone continues to perform as well as expected, it could mean a huge reduction in the training time for police dogs, which usually takes about five years.
科学家的最终目标是克隆出优秀的警犬，使其训练时间从数年缩短到数月。
The ultimate goal of scientists is to produce clones of talented police dogs that can be trained in months instead of years.
然而，由于目前这项技术的成本，这一目标尚无法实现。
However, this goal is not yet possible due to the current costs of the technology.
这并非首次克隆出明星警犬。
This is not the first time a clone has been made of a star police dog.
在韩国，六只克隆犬于 2008 年开始与警方合作。
In South Korea, six clones began working with the police in 2008.
