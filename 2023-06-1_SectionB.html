I have one that I use to cover my phone screen too.

Blue light includes natural light, but it also includes light that isn't natural.

Blue light isn't necessarily bad for us.

In fact, we need blue light during the day to be healthy.

我不太确定睡眠是否真像人们常说的那么重要。
I'm not so sure that sleep is nearly as important as people always say it is.

And it's the same for most of my friends.

嗯，我读过的研究表明，睡眠可能比我们以为的还要重要，而且睡眠不足会导致肥胖、心脏病等严重的健康问题。

on the conversation you have just heard.

My daughter's stock answers are basketball player, pop singer, mechanical engineer.

Adults love that last one, as it's the perfect mix of the sensible and the ambitious.

When she was much younger, my daughter used to say she wanted to be queen of the clouds, which I loved.
这才是我希望在孩子身上看到的立目标方式，源自他们天马行空的想象。
That's the kind of goal-setting I like to see in children, springing from their boundless imaginations.
是啊，我们成年人对现实的需求会显得乏味而狭隘，我们传递的是一种非常灰暗的成年形象：无论孩子的未来是什么样，都必须放在一份工作的框架里来看。
Yes, we grown-ups can be tedious and limiting in our need for reality, and we teach a very gloomy image of adulthood, that whatever our children's future holds, it must be seen within the context of a job.
这多么令人喘不过气，多么乏味。
How utterly overwhelming and dull.
当人们问我儿子长大后想做什么时，我得强忍住冲动，不去说：嘿，别打搅我孩子的梦想。
When people ask my son what he wants to be when he grows up, I have to swallow the urge to say, hey, back off my kid's dreams.
我们不能否认，青少年得为毕业后做点什么做好打算，父母也有权期望这不只是每天花 10 个小时打电脑游戏。
We can't dismiss the idea that teenagers have to plan to do something after they finish school, and parents are entitled to hope it's more than simply spending 10 hours a day playing computer games.
但问一句你想当什么，
But asking, what do you want to be?
并不会让孩子过上充实的人生，反而会带来不切实际的期望，以及很大概率的失望。
isn't going to lead a child to a fulfilled life, rather it leads to false expectations and a high chance of disappointment.
正是如此。
Exactly.
我们应该帮助孩子了解自己是谁，即使这意味着要放下我们认为他们应该成为的样子。
We should be helping our kids understand who they are, even if that means letting go of who we think they should be.
