Passage 2.
越来越多的人开始从事单板滑雪、滑板、山地自行车等种种极限运动。
More and more people are taking up extreme sports like snowboarding, skateboarding, mountain biking and many more.
极限运动最擅长的也许就是帮你不再恐惧。
One thing that extreme sports do better than perhaps anything else is to help you stop being afraid.
说它们的主要作用是引发恐惧，听起来也许很奇怪。
It may sound strange to say that their main function is to induce fear.
但过一段时间后，你对生活中其他事物的恐惧反应会大幅减弱。
But after a time, your fear response to other things in life will be dramatically reduced.
毕竟，如果你能从飞机上或桥上跳下去，那其他任何事你都能轻松面对。
After all, if you can jump out of a plane or off a bridge, then you can face anything else easily.
从事极限运动时，你必须更加专注。
When doing extreme sports, you have to become more focused.
你会被逼到极限，而如果你不够专注，就会犯下危险的错误。
You'll be pushed to your limits, and if you aren't focused, you'll make dangerous mistakes.
在享受极限运动时学会这样专注，会帮助你在工作中保持专注，让你更高效，也最终更成功。
Learning to be this focused when enjoying extreme sports will help you to be focused at work, keeping you more productive and ultimately more successful.
保持健康当然很好，但常规的锻炼方式和运动只会反复锻炼同样的肌肉。
It's great to stay fit and healthy, but standard exercise routines and sports only work the same muscles repeatedly.
而从事极限运动，你会用到完全不同的肌肉，这意味着你能得到全身锻炼。
With extreme sports, you'll be working entirely different muscles, and that means you get an all -over workout.
极限运动也比其他运动消耗更多的热量。
Extreme sports also burn a lot more calories than other sports.
例如，滑板每小时可以消耗多达 500 卡路里。
Skateboarding, for example, can burn as many as 500 calories per hour.
打篮球在同样时间里消耗约 300 卡路里。
Basketball burns around 300 in the same time.
当你发现自己能够克服极限运动带来的身体或心理挑战时，你会感觉自己超乎常人，自信心也会达到前所未有的高度。
When you find that you can overcome the physical or mental challenges involved in extreme sports, you'll feel superhuman, and your self-confidence will be at an all-time high.

Passage 3.

冲突是自然的。
Conflict is natural.

我们都有不同的性格偏好和倾向。
We all have different personality preferences and tendencies.

但很多时候，我们会陷入这样一种境地：冲突被视为负面的事情，是我们为了维持职场和谐而想要避免的东西。
But too often, we get caught in this place where conflict is perceived to be negative, something we want to avoid so that we can maintain the harmony of our workplace.
这可能是因为有些人想不惜一切代价避免冲突。
This could be because some people want to avoid conflict at all costs.
毕竟，他们还得一起共事。
After all, they still have to work together.
但这种人为的和谐并不是解决问题的办法。
But this kind of artificial harmony isn't the answer.
对于想要推进并做出更多成绩的团队和组织来说，富有成效的冲突是至关重要的一部分。
Productive conflict is a vital part of teams and organisations that want to push forward and do more.
没有冲突，我们常常会困在人为的和谐之中，人们因为担心可能与他人起冲突而不敢表达可能具有创新性的想法。
Without conflict, we're often stuck in this artificial harmony where people don't express potentially innovative ideas for fear that they may start conflict with others.
但如果你身处一个有信任基础的环境，冲突可以极其富有成效。
But if you're in a place where you have a basis of trust, conflict can be extremely productive.
它能带来更强的创新和团队中更大的信任。
It can lead to increased innovation and greater trust on teams.
这可能是一个令人不适的过程，但优秀的领导者和健康的团队都认识到，富有成效且健康的冲突是他们运作方式的重要组成部分。
It may be an uncomfortable process, but good leaders and healthy teams recognise that productive and healthy conflict is an important part of how they function.
