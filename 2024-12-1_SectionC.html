短文一。
Passage 1.
孩子们每天都需要时间跑一跑、跳一跳、伸伸胳膊、玩一玩。
Kids need time every day to run, jump, stretch, and play.
研究显示，这些经历能培养孩子在体育活动中的自信与乐趣，发展他们的运动技能，甚至改善情绪健康。
These experiences have been shown to build children's confidence and pleasure in physical activities, develop their motor skills, and even improve emotional well-being.
首先，孩子似乎天生渴望克服挑战、承担风险。
To begin with, children seem to have a natural desire to overcome challenges and take risks.
通过身体活动去冒健康的险，能增强孩子的自信、解决问题的能力，以及在挫败中坚持下去的韧性。
Taking healthy risks through physical movement builds children's confidence and ability to solve problems and persist through frustration.
其次，运动能锻炼孩子的大肌肉技能，比如协调性和平衡能力，也能锻炼他们完成写字、系鞋带、抛接球等任务所需的精细动作技能。
Secondly, movement activities build children's big-body skills, such as coordination and balance, as well as the fine motor skills they need for tasks like writing, tying their shoes, or throwing and catching a ball.
第三，据美国心理学会，规律的体育活动——尤其是户外活动——能减轻孩子的压力和抑郁，并提升他们专注和学习的能力。
Thirdly, according to the American Psychological Association, regular physical activity, and especially outdoor activity, reduces children's stress and depression and improves their ability to focus and learn.
规律锻炼能显著提升自我调节能力，减少因不良行为受纪律处分的次数。
Regular exercise can significantly improve self-regulation and decrease disciplinary consequences for negative behavior.
体育活动为挫败、焦虑或愤怒提供了积极的宣泄渠道，并能成为受用一生的健康应对方式。
Physical activity provides a positive outlet for frustration, anxiety, or anger, and can become a healthy coping skill throughout life.
最后，我们知道体育活动对身心健康都很重要，而运动习惯的培养要趁早。
Finally, we know that physical activity is important for our physical and mental health, and cultivating the habit of physical activity starts early.
若童年早期常有积极的运动体验，孩子更容易终身热爱运动。
Children are more likely to develop a lifelong love of physical activity from frequent positive early experiences.
并非每个孩子都喜欢竞技运动或玩球，这没关系。
Not every child enjoys competitive sports or playing with balls, and that's okay.
还有很多别的选择，比如想象游戏、非竞技类游戏，以及园艺或亲近自然的活动。
There are plenty of other options, such as imaginative play, non-competitive games, and gardening or nature experiences.
短文二。
Passage 2.
一项新研究发现，当公司更加多元化时，求职者甚至愿意考虑薪水更低的工作。
A new study finds that job seekers are even willing to explore jobs with lower salaries when companies are more diverse.
研究发现，公开多元化信息会让招聘启事对求职者更有吸引力，即便薪水更低。
It finds that sharing information about diversity makes job postings more attractive to job seekers, even when pay is lower.
为开展这项研究，团队与一家在线招聘平台合作，该平台会根据个人情况和求职条件推送招聘信息。
To conduct the study, the team partnered with an online job listings platform, which emails postings relevant to individuals and their job search criteria.
在为期 11 周的研究中，平台向约 18 万名用户发送招聘信息，用户被分为两组。
For the course of the 11-week study, the platform emailed job listings to some 180 ,000 users placed in one of two conditions.
在对照组中，参与者看到的是普通格式的邮件，不含任何多元化信息。
In the baseline condition, participants saw emails in the normal format, with no information on diversity.
在多元化组中，参与者会看到每条招聘信息的多元化评分，包括该公司员工的种族、性别、教育程度和语言技能等信息，并与同行业、同地区的其他公司作比较。
In the diversity condition, participants saw a diversity score for each listing, information about the race, gender, education, and language skills of a company's workforce, compared to others in the same sector and location.
随后收集了每封邮件是否被打开、每位用户点击了哪些招聘信息等数据。
Information was then gathered on whether the email was opened and which job listings were clicked on by each user.
相比对照组，多元化组的参与者更倾向于点击多元化评分略高的公司的招聘信息。
Participants in the diversity condition tended to click on job listings from companies with slightly higher diversity scores than those in the baseline condition.
也就是说，参与者似乎在关注多元化信息，并据此决定是否点击该信息。
That is, participants seemed to be paying attention to information about diversity and using this when deciding whether to click on the listing.
团队还发现，如果来自多元化评分较高的公司，参与者愿意去看薪水更低的招聘信息。
And the team also found that participants were willing to look at listings with lower salaries, if they came from companies with a higher diversity score.
短文三。
Passage 3.
俄勒冈州立大学的一项新研究发现，狗会倾向于模仿家中孩子的行为。
A new study from Oregon State University found that dogs tend to match their behaviour with the children in their family.
这一发现很重要，因为越来越多的证据表明，狗能在很多方面帮助孩子。
The findings are important because there is a growing body of evidence that dogs can help children in many ways.
这些帮助包括促进社交发展、增加身体活动、缓解焦虑，或在家庭结构变化时成为情感依恋的来源。
These include helping with social development, increasing physical activity, managing anxiety, or serving as a source of attachment in the face of changing family structures.
然而，很少有研究关注狗如何感知孩子、如何与孩子进行社交互动。
Yet very little research has focused on how dogs perceive and socially engage with children.
该研究第一作者莫妮克·乌德尔说，好消息是，这项研究表明狗非常关注和自己一起生活的孩子。
The great news is that this study suggests dogs are paying a lot of attention to the kids that they live with, said Monique Oudel, the lead author of the study.
我们观察到一件有趣的事：狗会模仿孩子的行为，但频率低于狗与成人之间的情况。
One interesting thing we have observed is that dogs are matching children's behaviour, but less frequently than what we have seen between dogs and adults.
这说明，虽然狗可能把孩子当作社交伙伴，但其中仍有一些差异需要我们进一步了解。
This suggests that while dogs may view children as social companions, there are also some differences that we need to understand better.
尽管研究发现狗能对孩子的成长产生很多积极影响，但孩子与狗的关系中也存在风险。
While research has found dogs can have a lot of positive impacts on a child's life, there are also risks associated with a dog -child relationship.
例如，其他研究发现，狗咬孩子的可能性高于咬成人。
For example, other studies have found dogs are more likely to bite children versus adults.
乌德尔说，关于孩子与狗的关系，我们还有很多要学，但研究确实表明，孩子完全有能力训练狗。
We still have a lot to learn about the dog-child relationship, Oudel said, but research does show that kids are very capable of training dogs.
