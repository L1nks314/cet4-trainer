短文一。
Passage 1.
发射升空通常是任何太空任务中对环境危害最大的阶段，大量燃料在几分钟内就被烧掉。
Liftoff is usually the most environmentally harmful stage of any space mission, with vast quantities of fuel burnt up in a matter of minutes.
例如，SpaceX 的猎鹰 9 号要消耗 112 吨精炼燃料，排放约 336 吨二氧化碳。
For instance, SpaceX's Falcon 9 gets through 112 tons of refined fuel, emitting about 336 tons of CO2.
这相当于一辆普通汽车几乎绕地球行驶 70 圈所排放的量。
This is the equivalent produced by your average car driving almost 70 times around the world.
除了温室气体，火箭发动机还会排放破坏臭氧的颗粒物。
As well as greenhouse gases, rocket engines emit particles that destroy ozone.
随着商业航天的兴起，这些问题正变得越来越紧迫。
These issues are growing more pressing with the emergence of commercial spaceflight.
2020 年有 114 次太空发射，但未来每年可能多达 1000 次。
There were 114 space launches in 2020, but there may be up to 1,000 per year in future.
可持续燃料是实现更环保太空旅行的首要任务。
Sustainable fuels are the top priority to enable greener space travel.
目前的航天器使用多种燃料，但大多以化石燃料为基础。
Current spacecraft use a variety of fuels, but most are based on fossil fuels.
一种可能更环保的选择是液氢和液氧。
One potentially greener option is liquid hydrogen and oxygen.
利用太阳能把水分解成氧气和氢气，就能可持续地获得氢。
Hydrogen can be obtained sustainably by using solar power to break water down into oxygen and hydrogen.
可重复使用的火箭能减少一些与太空飞行相关的浪费。
Reusable rockets can cut down on some of the waste associated with spaceflight.
传统上，助推器、燃料箱和其他部件都被视为一次性消耗品，但以可控方式把它们引导回地球带来了新的可能。
Traditionally, boosters, fuel tanks and other components are treated as expendable, but guiding them back to Earth in a controlled manner opens new possibilities.
猎鹰 9 号的大部分部件最多可重复使用 100 次。
Most components from the Falcon 9 can be reused up to 100 times.
真正环保的太空旅行还有一段路要走，但我们已经拥有许多开始限制其对地球影响所需的技术。
Truly environmentally friendly space travel is still some way off, but we already have many of the technologies needed to start limiting its impact on our planet.
短文二。
Passage 2.
帮着照看宠物能促进儿童情感发展的许多方面。
Helping to look after a pet can facilitate numerous aspects of a child's emotional development.
研究表明，对着狗或猫这样忠诚而又不加评判的伙伴朗读，能鼓励不爱阅读的孩子大声读书，这反过来又会增强他们的自信心。
Research shows that reading to a loyal and non-judgmental companion, such as a dog or cat, can encourage reluctant readers to read aloud, which will in turn boost their self-confidence.
有一个可以倾诉情感的伙伴，也能帮助孩子感到安心，并培养沟通能力。
Having a companion to talk to about their feelings can also help children feel secure and develop communication skills.
当父母讲解让宠物保持快乐健康所需的步骤时，家养宠物也能培养孩子的责任感。
A household pet can also cultivate a child's sense of responsibility as their parent explains the necessary processes of keeping their pet happy and healthy.
随着孩子长大，他们就能承担更多日常事务，从而增强独立意识。
As a child matures, they can then take on more of the daily responsibilities, boosting their sense of independence.
宠物还为研究和学习提供了绝佳的主题。
Pets also provide a fantastic subject for research and learning.
父母可以鼓励孩子在图书馆或在有人监督的上网搜索中研究他们最喜欢的宠物。
Parents can encourage their child to research their favorite pets at the library or during a supervised internet search.
带孩子去宠物店购买食物和用品，能在他们求知欲旺盛之时促进他们的认知发展。
Taking children along to the pet shop to buy food and supplies will support their cognitive development as their passion for learning flourishes.
在新鲜空气中遛宠物，对孩子的身体发育大有裨益。
A child's physical development can benefit greatly from taking pets for walks in the fresh air.
在照顾心爱动物的过程中，甚至能锻炼他们的精细动作技能。
Even their fine motor skills can be utilized in taking care of a beloved animal.
当责任安排得当并事先说明时，孩子参与养育一只快乐健康的宠物会受益匪浅。
When responsibilities are well paced and communicated, a child can benefit greatly from involvement in bringing up a happy and healthy pet.
当然，如果孩子自己家里没有宠物，他们去有宠物的朋友家做客时，同样能获得上述所有好处。
And of course, if a child doesn't have a pet in their own household, they can still benefit from all of the above when visiting a friend who does.
短文三。
Passage 3.
俄亥俄州立法机构的一项新法案将要求州立公立高校向准大学生寄送一份信息资料。
A new bill in the Ohio legislature would require state public colleges and universities to send prospective students a packet of information.
其中将包括大学费用、助学金和奖学金。
This would include costs of college, grants, and scholarships.
还将包括预期的每月贷款还款额和毕业后的预计薪资。
It would also include expected monthly loan payments and projected salary after college.
州众议员亚当·马修斯是提出该法案的两名议员之一。
State Representative Adam Matthews is one of the two legislators who proposed the bill.
他说，这会让准大学生对毕业后的情况有更多了解。
He said it would give prospective students more information about what to expect once they graduate.
马修斯说，把这些信息呈现在他们面前，将给他们最好的机会，让他们的技能和梦想与我们的学校所能提供的东西相匹配。
Putting that information in front of them will give them the best opportunity to match their skills and their dreams to what our schools can provide, said Matthews.
他指出，州政府只能以这种方式监管公立机构，但他希望俄亥俄州的私立机构也能效仿州政府的做法，公布类似的信息。
He notes that the state can only regulate public institutions in this way, but he hopes private institutions in Ohio follow the lead of the state and post similar information.
他认为，公布这些信息将有助于俄亥俄州的高校更具竞争力。
He believes giving out this information will help Ohio's colleges and universities to be more competitive.
这些信息将凸显它们以非常合理的费用提供了极高的价值。
The information will highlight what a great value they have for their very reasonable cost.
俄亥俄州的学校本来就很有竞争力。
Ohio's schools are already competitive.
该州吸引来的本科生比它送往州外高校的还要多。
The state attracts more undergraduate students than it sends to colleges out of state.
目前许多高校都在其网站上提供类似的信息。
Many colleges and universities currently provide similar information on their websites.
这包括学费、杂费、毕业率等等。
This includes tuition, fees, graduation rates, and more.
但学生需要自己去查找这些信息。
But students need to search for the information.
这项法案将要求高校直接把资料寄给学生。
This bill would require the universities and colleges to send a packet directly to students.
