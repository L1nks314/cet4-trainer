题型：给下面 10 个句子各配一个段落字母（A–M）。 先读句子划关键词，再回原文定位。原文见左侧，题目在右侧。

纽约时报

13 段 · 10 题
A

This fall, thousands of college students from across the country will begin their undergraduate studies at colleges around the nation. They will inevitably pack too much to fit in their tiny dorm rooms. They will also carry with them a huge student loan debt, in addition to countless "hidden" out-of-pocket costs paid for by their bank accounts and the bank accounts of their families.

今年秋天，来自全国各地的数千名大学生将在全国各地的大学开始他们的本科学习。他们不可避免地会收拾得太多，无法容纳在狭小的宿舍里。除了由银行账户和家人的银行账户支付的无数“隐藏”自付费用外，他们还将背负巨额学生贷款债务。

B

At my well-respected, private, four-year university in Washington, D.C., which boasts a yearly tuition of $44,046 not including room and board, I receive over $57,000 yearly in financial aid. As a student from a family that is struggling to make ends meet, my financial aid package is a combination of federal grants and federal work study, university merit scholarships and financial aid awards, and about $8,000 yearly in federally subsidized(有补贴的) and unsubsidized loans. On paper, my expenses and my financial aid just about even out. Off paper, they don't.

我在华盛顿特区备受尊敬的私立四年制大学每年的学费为44,046 $ ，不包括食宿，我每年收到超过57,000 $的财政援助。作为一名来自努力维持生计的家庭的学生，我的财政援助方案是联邦补助金和联邦工作学习，大学优秀奖学金和财政援助奖励的组合，每年约有8,000 $的联邦补贴（有补贴的 ）和无补贴贷款。在纸面上，我的费用和经济援助几乎是均衡的。不在纸上，他们没有。

C

Universities today are in the business of making money, and mine is no exception. They hit me right out of the gate with a $160 fee to attend my freshman orientation, a price which does not include the cost of travel to and from the District. Almost every class has an associated fee not included in the cost of tuition, most between $40 and $100. Fees for lab science classes are the highest, and all students at my university are required to take at least one lab before they graduate. Buying a laptop proved a necessity and, thankfully, a relative bought me one as a gift. Renting a mini-fridge for my dorm room costs my roommates and me about $140 a year.

今天的大学都在赚钱，我的大学也不例外。他们直接向我收取了160 $的费用，以参加我的新生入学培训，该价格不包括往返该地区的旅行费用。几乎每个课程都有不包含在学费中的相关费用，大多数在$ 40到$ 100之间。实验室科学课程的费用最高，我大学的所有学生都必须在毕业前至少参加一个实验室。事实证明，买笔记本电脑是必要的，谢天谢地，一位亲戚给我买了一台笔记本电脑作为礼物。我的宿舍房间租一台迷你冰箱，我和室友每年花费约140 $。

D

Schools will charge you whatever they can. The costs of any damage to the dorm, including elevators, bathrooms, and common areas, are billed to every person on a dorm floor, or even the entire building if they do not know who caused the damage. After I fell out of my bed twice during my freshman year, the university installed a railing—for $20, billed to my student account. My financial aid did not anticipate any of these costs, and so it did not cover them.

学校会尽其所能向您收费。如果宿舍（包括电梯、卫生间和公共区域）受到任何损坏，我们会向宿舍楼层的每个人收取费用，如果他们不知道谁造成了损坏，甚至会向整栋大楼收取费用。大一期间我两次从床上摔下来后，大学安装了一个20 $的栏杆，记在我的学生账户上。我的经济援助没有预料到任何这些费用，所以它没有支付这些费用。

E

An Internet search of "hidden costs" of college turns up a host of articles on parent-centered websites on the college application process. These articles are almost always geared towards upper- and middle-class families. For students already struggling to pay tuition, these costs may be the least of their worries.

在互联网上搜索大学的“隐藏费用” ，发现以家长为中心的网站上有很多关于大学申请流程的文章。这些文章几乎总是针对上层和中产阶级家庭。对于已经难以支付学费的学生来说，这些费用可能是他们最不担心的。

F

So what hidden costs should low-income students really be paying attention to? My college experience offers a few examples.

那么，低收入学生真正应该关注的隐性成本是什么？我的大学经历提供了一些例子。

G

If you are a low-income student who will be attending school out-of-state, make sure you know if you can use your state benefits, such as the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program, or SNAP. It wasn't until after I had accepted admission to an out-of-state school that I learned that I could not use my Ohio Medicaid on campus for anything other than emergency care. My benefits became invalid the second I moved out of Ohio. After my freshman year, I had to opt for the school's insurance plan, which costs around $2,000 a year. Even if your school offers a flat-rate fee for a doctor's appointment at the student health center (mine is $20 a visit), these fees often do not include extra fees for lab tests or prescription medications(药物).

如果您是将在州外上学的低收入学生，请确保您知道是否可以使用您的州福利，例如补充营养援助计划或SNAP。直到我接受了州外学校的录取，我才知道我不能在校园里将我的俄亥俄州医疗补助用于紧急护理以外的任何事情。从我搬出俄亥俄州的那一刻起，我的福利就失效了。大一毕业后，我不得不选择学校的保险计划，每年的费用约为2000 $。即使您的学校为学生健康中心的医生预约提供统一费用（我的费用为每次20 $ ） ，这些费用通常不包括实验室检查或处方药的额外费用（药物 ）。

H

If you plan on paying off bills in your student account with a credit card, be aware of any additional costs. My school charges an additional fee for the use of a credit card to settle outstanding charges, which can add upwards of 3% of the balance to your bill.

如果您计划使用信用卡在学生账户中支付账单，请注意任何额外费用。我的学校对使用信用卡结清未付费用收取额外费用，这可以将余额的3 ％添加到您的账单中。

I

There is another depressing reality hidden within even the largest financial aid packages: Colleges often offer the most generous packages during freshman year as a way to attract new students. My family was careful to ask about the chances of financial aid being taken away after my freshman year. We were assured that, except for low grades or a raise in family income, no money would be taken away. We did not know to ask—and the school did not readily point out—that even if tuition rises, my financial aid package will stay the same. So when my university voted to raise tuition costs 3% at the end of my freshman year, my financial aid package remained the same and I was suddenly responsible for an additional $1,200 for the next year. The university administration will likely vote to raise costs at least once more before my graduation.

即使是最大的经济援助计划中也隐藏着另一个令人沮丧的现实：大学通常在大一期间提供最慷慨的计划，以吸引新学生。我的家人小心翼翼地询问我大一后经济援助被取消的可能性。我们得到保证，除了低年级或家庭收入增加外，不会拿走任何钱。我们不知道问--学校也不容易指出--即使学费上涨，我的财政援助方案也将保持不变。因此，当我的大学在我一年级结束时投票决定提高学费3 ％时，我的经济援助计划保持不变，我突然为下一年额外的1,200 $负责。在我毕业之前，大学管理部门可能会投票决定至少再提高一次成本。

J

Yes, I chose to attend an expensive university far from my hometown. Yes, there were cheaper options. But there are promising students from struggling families across the nation who should not rule out their dream schools entirely. All things considered, I am paying significantly less than the ticket price of my university, and having an educational experience in Washington, D.C., that I would not have had anywhere else. As a low-income student from a down-and-out Rust Belt community, these educational experiences have enormous potential to brighten my future—and my family's future.

是的，我选择上远离家乡的一所昂贵的大学。是的，还有更便宜的选择。但是，来自全国各地苦苦挣扎的家庭的有前途的学生不应该完全排除他们梦寐以求的学校。考虑到所有因素，我支付的费用远低于我大学的门票价格，并且在华盛顿特区有过教育经历，这是我在其他任何地方都没有过的。作为一名来自Rust Belt社区的低收入学生，这些教育经历具有巨大的潜力，可以照亮我的未来和家人的未来。

K

The key is to make sure that students and their families understand that hidden costs exist, and that they may prove problematic. Fill out a more comprehensive checklist, and be wary of listed prices that seem too low. Understand just how complicated the financial aid process is.

关键是要确保学生及其家人了解隐性成本的存在，并且这些成本可能会证明是有问题的。请填写更全面的检查清单，并警惕房源价格过低的情况。了解经济援助流程的复杂程度。

L

Students and families must also understand their ability to self-advocate. They should not pay student bills or excess fees blindly. If something does not look right, ask about it. If it still doesn't look right, negotiate it. In cases where parents are working multiple jobs, are less knowledgeable about college bureaucracies, have limited English language skills, or are not contributing financially to their child's education, the burden of self-advocacy will fall on the student. I understand the difficulty, and the embarrassment. But it is necessary.

学生和家庭还必须了解他们的自我倡导能力。他们不应该盲目支付学生账单或超额费用。如果某些东西看起来不对劲，请询问。如果看起来仍然不对，请协商。如果父母从事多种工作，对大学官僚机构的了解较少，英语语言技能有限，或者没有为孩子的教育做出经济贡献，自我宣传的负担将落在学生身上。我理解困难和尴尬。有必要

M

In the grand scheme of things, however, colleges also must come to understand that the hidden fees they ask for may prove unmanageable for the very kinds of low-income or first-generation students they are trying desperately to attract.

然而，在宏伟的计划中，大学也必须明白，他们所要求的隐藏费用对于他们拼命想要吸引的低收入或第一代学生来说可能是无法控制的。
