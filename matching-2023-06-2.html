2023-06-2 长篇阅读

14 段 · 10 题
A

For the past four decades, the leader of Formula One car racing, one of the biggest annual sporting series in the world, was Bernie Ecclestone, a former motorcycle parts dealer who built it into an international presence essentially on his own.

在过去的四十年里，世界上最大的年度体育赛事之一的一级方程式赛车的领导者是前摩托车零部件经销商伯尼·埃克莱斯顿（ Bernie Ecclestone ） ，他基本上是靠自己将其打造成国际形象的。

B

A skilled backroom operator who speaks without a filter, Ecclestone said often that in his opinion, the sport was at its best when he was allowed to act as "a dictator."

埃克莱斯顿是一位技术娴熟的幕后操作员，在没有过滤器的情况下说话，他经常说，在他看来，当他被允许充当“独裁者”时，这项运动处于最佳状态。

C

Yet now the dictator is gone. After an American company, Liberty Media, acquired the Formula One competition recently, Chase Carey—a former executive with Fox Broadcasting Company and DirecTV who by his own admission is not a fierce racing fan—was named to replace Ecclestone and to try to renovate the organization's management, reach and ambition.

然而现在独裁者已经走了。在美国公司Liberty Media最近收购了一级方程式比赛之后， Chase Carey--福克斯广播公司和DirecTV的前高管，他自己也承认自己不是一个凶猛的赛车迷-被命名为取代Ecclestone ，并试图翻新该组织的管理，影响力和雄心壮志。

D

Among the goals, Carey said in an interview on Tuesday, is one that just about every global sport seems interested in chasing: increasing interest in the United States. "People have said we're going to 'Americanize' it," Carey said. "And we're not going to do that totally. But realistically, there are some elements of Americanization that the sport could use."

凯里在周二的一次采访中说，在这些目标中，几乎所有全球体育运动似乎都对追逐感兴趣：对美国的兴趣越来越大。“人们说我们要把它'美国化' ，”凯里说。“我们不会完全做到这一点。但实际上，这项运动可以使用美国化的一些元素。”

E

While Formula One commands enormous audiences throughout much of the world, many American sports fans know it as that other motorsport, the one that is not Nascar(纳斯卡车赛). Formula One teams race far more technologically advanced vehicles around tracks all over the world—in magnificent events in places like Malaysia, Monaco, Singapore and the United Arab Emirates, and on tradition-rich tracks like Silverstone in England and Monza in Italy too.

虽然一级方程式在世界大部分地区拥有庞大的观众，但许多美国体育迷将其称为另一项赛车运动，即不是纳斯卡的赛车运动（纳斯卡车赛 ）。一级方程式车队在世界各地的赛道上进行着技术更先进的赛车比赛--在马来西亚、摩纳哥、新加坡和阿拉伯联合酋长国等地举办的盛大赛事，以及在英格兰的银石赛道和意大利的蒙扎赛道上进行的盛大赛事。

F

The series has an annual race in Austin, Texas. But within "a few years," Carey said, he plans to bring another to a destination American city, like New York, Los Angeles, Miami or Las Vegas. Carey's ambitious plan is two-fold: first, change the business model of Formula One, which he said was a "one-man show" under Ecclestone that had a largely narrow vision when it came to negotiating partnership deals; and second, alter the way fans experience the sport, both in person and remotely, so that connections between the audience and people within the series are easier to make.

该系列赛每年在德克萨斯州奥斯汀举行。但凯里说，在“几年内” ，他计划将另一个带到纽约、洛杉矶、迈阿密或拉斯维加斯等美国目的地城市。凯里雄心勃勃的计划有两个方面：首先，改变一级方程式的商业模式，他说这是埃克莱斯顿的“单人秀” ，在谈判合作伙伴协议时视野很狭窄；其次，改变粉丝亲自和远程体验这项运动的方式，以便更容易建立观众和系列赛中的人之间的联系。

G

Increased digital access for fans, a more behind-the-scenes experience for broadcast viewers and innovation in areas like virtual reality—what is it like to speed around a track inside a Ferrari?—are among the possibilities. "The sport has clearly been underserved," Carey said. "It doesn't do anything digitally. There's no marketing. It doesn't tell any stories. The goal in this is to make the fans connect to the live experience as much as possible, and the tools you have to do that, we're not using at all."

增加了球迷的数字接入，为广播观众提供了更多的幕后体验，以及虚拟现实等领域的创新--在法拉利赛道上加速是什么感觉？“这项运动显然服务不足，”凯里说。“它不做任何数字化工作。没有市场营销。它没有讲述任何故事。这样做的目的是让粉丝尽可能多地与现场体验联系起来，而您必须这样做的工具，我们根本不使用。”

H

The larger question, though, is a familiar one: Is there room for Formula One in the ever-crowded sports landscape of the United States? Opinions vary, particularly because viewing habits among consumers continue to evolve. John Bloom, a professor at Shippensburg University who has studied American sports history, said the biggest challenge for any sport trying to increase its presence in the United States was framing itself in a way that had lasting appeal. "Sports generally become popular in some way because they establish a narrative," Bloom said. "When I think of motorsports in the U.S., what we all think of is Nascar, and the narrative of Nascar is sort of rural, white, working-class Americans, mostly in the South, connecting with the atmosphere of those races. That's the narrative. When I think of the narrative of Formula One, it's a very different kind of audience."

然而，更大的问题是一个熟悉的问题：在美国日益拥挤的体育格局中，一级方程式是否有空间？意见各不相同，特别是因为消费者的观看习惯在不断变化。希彭斯堡大学（ Shippensburg University ）研究美国体育史的教授约翰·布鲁姆（ John Bloom ）表示，任何试图增加其在美国的影响力的体育运动面临的最大挑战都是以一种具有持久吸引力的方式构建自己。“体育运动通常以某种方式变得流行，因为它们建立了叙事，”布鲁姆说。“当我想到美国的赛车运动时，我们都会想到纳斯卡，纳斯卡的叙述是农村，白人，工薪阶层的美国人，主要在南方，与这些比赛的气氛联系在一起。这就是故事的来龙去脉。当我想到一级方程式的叙述时，它是一种非常不同的观众。”

I

That difference, Carey said, is significant. While some might immediately link Formula One to Nascar in terms of American growth, Carey said Formula One's brand research had indicated there was very little crossover; rather, Formula One fans generally cite other so-called elite events, like Wimbledon or the Ryder Cup, as competitions they enjoy. "Other than they're both cars, the Nascar fan base is a very different fan base," Carey said. "It's a very regional fan base. Formula One is a global, famous brand of stars. These are machines that shock and awe you."

Carey说，这种差异是显着的。虽然有些人可能会立即将一级方程式赛车与纳斯卡在美国的增长联系起来，但凯里表示，一级方程式赛车的品牌研究表明，跨界赛车很少；相反，一级方程式赛车迷通常将温布尔登或莱德杯等其他所谓的精英赛事作为他们喜欢的比赛。“除了这两辆车之外，纳斯卡的粉丝群是一个非常不同的粉丝群，”凯里说。“这是一个非常区域性的粉丝群。一级方程式赛车是一个全球知名的明星品牌。这些机器让你感到震惊和敬畏。”

J

Carey's background is in deal making and innovation. At Fox Broadcasting Company, he was a top advisor for years, known for his skill in helping to lead the launch of the company into sports, as well as the start of Fox News Channel. After going to DirecTV, he positioned the satellite provider as a mainstream option in millions of households.

Carey的背景是交易制定和创新。在福克斯广播公司（ Fox Broadcasting Company ） ，他多年来一直担任高级顾问，以帮助公司进入体育界以及福克斯新闻频道（ Fox News Channel ）开播而闻名。在进入DirecTV后，他将这家卫星提供商定位为数百万家庭的主流选择。

K

Now, after Liberty Media paid $4.4 billion to acquire Formula One, he is charged with making the investment pay off. "I think they can build Formula One in the U.S.," said Patrick Crakes, an executive at InVivo Media Group who spent 25 years at Fox Broadcasting Company before leaving in 2016 as a senior manager at Fox Sports. "People don't work on their cars anymore. They don't want that connection anymore. It's about technology and pushing the limits. It's about speed, danger and risk. And Formula One has that more than any other racing series."

现在，在Liberty Media支付44 $收购一级方程式赛车后，他被指控使投资得到回报。“我认为他们可以在美国建立一级方程式赛车，” InVivo Media Group的高管帕特里克·克雷克斯（ Patrick Crakes ）说，他在福克斯广播公司（ Fox Broadcasting Company ）工作了25年，然后于2016年离开福克斯体育（ Fox Sports ）担任高级经理。“人们不再在汽车上工作了。他们不想再有这种联系了。这是关于技术和突破极限。这与速度、危险和风险有关。一级方程式比任何其他赛车系列都要多。”

L

That is what hooked Carey, and he said he thought his experience was not unusual. He recalled attending Formula One's Monaco race last year and being overwhelmed by the ceremony leading up to the event, the way the race charmed the city for days ahead of the start. In his mind, it felt like a Super Bowl(超级碗橄榄球赛).

这就是Carey着迷的原因，他说他认为自己的经历并不罕见。他回忆起去年参加一级方程式赛车的摩纳哥比赛，并被赛事前的仪式所震撼，比赛在开始前几天吸引了这座城市。在他看来，这就像一个超超级碗橄榄球赛级碗。

M

Then, on race day, he watched as the cars rocketed out of a tunnel and went screaming toward a tight turn with the city's harbor and the Mediterranean Sea in the background framing the scene. He was fascinated. "You can't help but be awed," he said, "and I think that feeling can be translated to the viewer."

然后，在比赛当天，他看着汽车从隧道中飙升，尖叫着朝着一个紧凑的转弯，城市的港口和地中海在背景中构成了场景。他着迷了。“你不禁感到敬畏，”他说， “我认为这种感觉可以转化为观众。”

N

He added: "The broader sport is a little too inward-looking, and we need to be more open. In some ways, I'm glad to be coming from the outside. The guys who are in the sport forever are sitting there saying: 'We can't do that. We can't do that because it's never been done that way.'"

他补充说： “更广泛的运动有点过于内向，我们需要更加开放。在某些方面，我很高兴能从外面来。永远参与这项运动的人坐在那里说： “我们不能这样做。我们不能这样做，因为从来没有那样做过。'”
