P1

P1 We all take a little extra effort to look nice for special occasions. But most of us have conflicting feelings about dressing up and feel guilty about taking the time to focus on clothes. Science now suggests the right dress may give ourselves the extra edge in our professional and personal lives.

P1我们都需要付出一些额外的努力，才能在特殊场合看起来很漂亮。但我们大多数人对打扮有矛盾的感觉，对花时间专注于衣服感到内疚。科学现在表明，合适的衣服可能会给我们的职业和个人生活带来额外的优势。

P2

P2 We hear sayings like "dress for the job you want; not the job you have". Most people don't really believe in them, but research into the impact of clothes on behavior now suggests that there may actually be a grain of truth in these sayings. Science says that the clothes we wear affect our behavior, our mood and even the way we interact with others because of the symbolic meaning that we assign to different types of clothing.

P2我们听到这样的说法： “为您想要的工作着装；而不是您所拥有的工作”。大多数人并不真正相信它们，但现在对衣服对行为的影响的研究表明，这些说法实际上可能有一点道理。科学说，我们穿的衣服会影响我们的行为，我们的情绪，甚至我们与他人互动的方式，因为我们赋予不同类型的衣服的象征意义。

P3

P3 We consider some clothes to be powerful, some to be fun, and so on. We even evaluate people whom we have just met based on their clothes. We also evaluate ourselves based on what we are wearing because of the way they make us feel. This means that the experience of wearing something affects our attitudes and our choice of behavior.

P3我们认为有些衣服很漂亮，有些很有趣，等等。我们甚至会根据刚认识的人的衣着对他们进行评估。我们还会根据自己穿着的方式来评估自己。这意味着穿着某件衣服的体验会影响我们的态度和行为选择。

P4

P4 There's a reason tailored jackets are associated with being 'dressed for success'. It seems that wearing formal office wear puts us in the right frame of mind to conduct business. Wearing power clothing makes us feel more confident and even increases hormones needed for displaying dominance. This in turn helps us become better negotiators and abstract thinkers.

P4定制夹克与“为成功着装”相关联是有原因的。穿着正式的办公服装似乎使我们在开展业务时处于正确的心态。穿着强力服装让我们感到更加自信，甚至增加了展现主导地位所需的激素。这反过来又帮助我们成为更好的谈判者和抽象思考者。

P5

P5 While a good suit works wonders for our performance in the boardroom, wearing formal wear isn't a great idea when we want to socialize. Studies have found that people tend to be less open and less able to relax when they wear formal clothes.

P5虽然一套好的西装可以为我们在董事会的表现创造奇迹，但当我们想要社交时，穿着正装并不是一个好主意。研究发现，人们穿着正式衣服时往往不太开放，也不太能放松。

P6

P6 On the other hand, a casual dress helps us become more friendly and creative. These findings support the idea of wearing business casuals on a Friday; since colleagues are most likely to take out time to socialize on the last work day of the week. I mean, who wants to hang out with people in their suits?

P6另一方面，休闲连衣裙有助于我们变得更加友好和富有创造力。这些发现支持在周五穿商务休闲装的想法；因为同事最有可能在一周的最后一个工作日抽出时间进行社交。我的意思是，谁想和穿着西装的人一起出去玩？

46.答案 A细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What does science suggest the right dress may do? 直接问 science 提出 dress 会做什么，属于针对原文某句客观事实的复述。

拆句

主干 What does science suggest the right dress may do? 是宾语从句嵌套：science 后跟省略 that 的宾语从句 the right dress may do What；理解上把 What 还原到从句末尾，即可看清问的是 dress 的功能/作用。

定位
P1

Science now suggests the right dress may give ourselves the extra edge in our professional and personal lives.

科学现在表明，穿对衣服可以在我们的职业与生活中给予我们额外的优势。

选项
A✓ 正确
the extra edge↔Add to our advantage
in our professional and personal lives↔in work and life

选项 Add to our advantage in work and life. 恰好覆盖「在职业与生活中增加优势」，与预判完全吻合。

B偷换概念

原文谈的是 the extra edge（优势），选项 Enable us to look a lot more attractive. 把它换成了外貌吸引力，方向不同。

C以偏概全

原文同时强调 professional and personal lives 两方面，选项 Help us to enjoy a fuller personal life. 只保留 personal life，丢掉了职业一面。

D偷换概念

原文是 the extra edge（优势），选项 Provide extra energy for what we do. 把 edge 换成 energy（精力），属于熟词僻义误读。

47.答案 D细节题展开解析
判型

题干 Why does science say the clothes one wears may affect their interaction with others? 问的是原因，原文应有 because/due to 等显式因果标志。

拆句

主干为 Why does science say … ；say 后是省略 that 的宾语从句 the clothes one wears may affect their interaction with others，其中 one wears 是修饰 clothes 的省略关系代词的定语从句，理解为「人们穿的衣服」。

定位
P2

Science says that the clothes we wear affect our behavior, our mood and even the way we interact with others because of the symbolic meaning that we assign to different types of clothing.

科学表明，我们所穿的衣服会影响我们的行为、情绪甚至我们与他人互动的方式，这是因为我们赋予不同类型衣物的象征意义。

选项
A无中生有

原文从未提及 social and economic status，Clothes usually represent one's social and economic status. 是凭印象添加。

B偷换概念

原文谈的是衣服带来的象征意义影响互动，选项 Clothes largely determine one's likability by people around. 把它替换为「决定一个人受不受人喜欢」，并加重为 largely determine。

C偷换概念

原文说衣服传递象征意义，选项 Different types of clothing markedly reflect different personalities. 把它换成「反映性格」，personalities 是原文没有的概念。

D✓ 正确
the symbolic meaning that we assign to different types of clothing↔Different types of clothing convey different messages symbolically.
assign↔convey

选项 Different types of clothing convey different messages symbolically. 把原文因果端逐一对应，是同义复述。

48.答案 B细节题展开解析
判型

题干 How do the clothes we wear sway our evaluation of ourselves? 问的是「通过什么方式」，答案对应原文一句解释机制的话。

拆句

题干主干 the clothes … sway our evaluation of ourselves；the clothes 后省略了 that，跟着定语从句 we wear，整体读作「我们所穿的衣服如何影响我们对自己的评价」。

定位
P3

We also evaluate ourselves based on what we are wearing because of the way they make us feel.

我们也会基于所穿衣服来评价自己，因为衣服会影响我们的感受。

选项
A偷换概念

原文 the way they make us feel 谈的是 feel（感受），选项 By exerting an effect on our power of judgment. 换成了 power of judgment（判断力），概念发生偏移。

B✓ 正确
the way they make us feel↔how we feel about ourselves
because of↔By impacting

选项 By impacting how we feel about ourselves. 把原文 the way they make us feel 用 how we feel about ourselves 改写，完全贴合预判。

C答非所问

选项 By affecting what we take as the basis for assessment. 描述的是「影响我们用什么作为评判依据」，等于反过来在说「clothes 改变了我们的评判标准」，可原文只说 clothes 让我们对自己有感受，再据此评价自己，并未谈「评判标准本身」。

D张冠李戴

symbolic messages 是第 2 段谈衣服影响互动的原因，选项 By influencing our interpretation of symbolic messages. 把它挪到了第 3 段评价自我的机制上，张冠李戴。

49.答案 D细节题展开解析
判型

Why does the author say tailored jackets are associated with being 'dressed for success'? 问作者为何这么说，正是细节原因题的典型表述。

拆句

主干 Why does the author say + 宾语从句 tailored jackets are associated with being 'dressed for success'；be associated with 是固定搭配「与……相关」，being 引导的动名词短语作介词宾语，整体读作「为什么作者说西装外套与『为成功而着装』相关」。

定位
P4

There's a reason tailored jackets are associated with being 'dressed for success'. It seems that wearing formal office wear puts us in the right frame of mind to conduct business.

西装外套之所以被认为与「为成功而着装」相关，是有原因的。看起来，穿正式办公着装会让我们进入做生意的正确心理状态。

选项
A过度推断

原文只说西装让人进入做生意心态，并未声称它是 necessary（必要条件），They are necessary for formal business dealings. 把「有帮助」拔高为「必备」。

B偷换概念

原文是 the right frame of mind（正确的心理状态），选项 They may help people concentrate on their business. 把它换成 concentrate（集中注意力），属于偷换。

C过度推断

原文 Wearing power clothing makes us feel more confident and even increases hormones needed for displaying dominance. 是讲附加效果，选项 They are vital to keeping a dominant position in business transactions. 把它拔高为「对保持优势地位至关重要」，既偏离了 Q49 问的「为何被称为 dressed for success」，又把程度上拉到 vital。

D✓ 正确
puts us in the right frame of mind↔enable people to have the right mentality
to conduct business↔for doing business

选项 They may enable people to have the right mentality for doing business. 把原文「让我们处于做生意的正确心态」逐一对应，是同义复述。

50.答案 C细节题展开解析
判型

What are people advised to do when they want to socialize? 询问具体建议行为，是典型的细节复述。

拆句

主干 What are people advised to do；when 引导时间状语从句 they want to socialize，整体读作「当人们想社交时，建议穿什么」。被动语态 are advised 隐含「文章作者建议」。

定位
P6

On the other hand, a casual dress helps us become more friendly and creative. These findings support the idea of wearing business casuals on a Friday; since colleagues are most likely to take out time to socialize on the last work day of the week.

另一方面，休闲着装能让我们变得更友好、更有创造力。这些发现支持周五穿商务休闲装的做法，因为同事们最有可能在一周的最后一个工作日抽时间社交。

选项
A答非所问

原文从未建议把注意力集中在「衣服」本身，Focus on clothing. 与「社交场合该穿什么」毫无对应。

B与原文相反

原文明说 wearing formal wear isn't a great idea when we want to socialize，选项 Wear a good suit. 恰好建议穿西装，与原文方向相反。

C✓ 正确
a casual dress↔Dress casually
wearing formal wear isn't a great idea when we want to socialize↔Dress casually

选项 Dress casually. 把 a casual dress 的建议浓缩为祈使句，完整对应原文。

D无中生有

原文从未提到「穿得与众不同」，Look unusual. 是凭空生造的建议。

第 2 篇 · 第 51–55 题

5 段 · 5 题
P1

With the rise of pop music, jazz, and electronic music, both opera and classical music started to fade away from the public eye. Some people are beginning to wonder whether opera and classical music are still relevant to the modern world of music. Granted, you will not typically see today's teenagers lending their ears to Bach anytime soon, but there are some major indicators that both opera and classical music are now still quite alive.

随着流行音乐、爵士乐和电子音乐的兴起，歌剧和古典音乐开始从公众视线中消失。有些人开始怀疑歌剧和古典音乐是否仍然与现代音乐世界相关。诚然，你通常不会很快看到今天的青少年向巴赫倾诉，但有一些主要指标表明，歌剧和古典音乐现在仍然相当活跃。

P2

The most major indicator of classical music's importance in society today is the fact that much of the popular music that is currently being produced uses similar beats, harmonies, and melodies(旋律) as those that were used in some of classical music's best works. Even so, it can be difficult for those who do not study music theory to see this as an indicator, since it is subtle and just shows the impact symphonic(交响乐的) orchestras have had on society's taste in music.

古典音乐在当今社会的重要性的最主要指标是，目前正在制作的大部分流行音乐都使用与一些古典音乐最佳作品中使用的节拍旋律、和声和旋律相似的节奏、和声和旋律。即便如此，对于那些不学习音乐理论的人来说，很难将其视为一个指标，因为它很微妙，只是展示了交交响乐的响乐团对社会音乐品味的影响。

P3

A better example for the relevance of opera and classical music can be seen in the invention of the rock opera. Opera, in its simplest definition, is telling a story using music as its form. The art of telling a story using music has not faded in the least bit. In fact, sometimes actual orchestras are used for major parts of the opera itself. Some of the world's greatest hits have been parts of rock operas.

在摇滚歌剧的发明中可以看到歌剧和古典音乐相关性的一个更好的例子。歌剧，用最简单的定义来说，就是以音乐为形式讲述一个故事。用音乐讲故事的艺术丝毫没有褪色。事实上，有时歌剧本身的主要部分会使用实际的管弦乐队。世界上一些最伟大的歌剧是摇滚歌剧的一部分。

P4

Fans of classical music can also tell you that there are few types of music that are more expressive. So, it should come as no surprise to anyone that classical music pieces are still used as background music in modern movies. Symphonic orchestra compositions have also been created solely for the purpose of being included in major motion pictures. These are often very well received amongst mainstream music fans.

古典音乐爱好者也可以告诉你，有几种类型的音乐更具表现力。因此，古典音乐作品在现代电影中仍然被用作背景音乐，这对任何人来说都不足为奇。交响乐团作品也完全是为了被纳入主要电影而创作的。这些通常在主流音乐爱好者中非常受欢迎。

P5

Classical music and opera are the very foundation of what our modern music is based upon. Considering the huge impact they have had on our current society, it is without doubt that we can expect them to continue to remain important for centuries to come.

古典音乐和歌剧是我们现代音乐的基础。考虑到它们对我们当前社会的巨大影响，毫无疑问，我们可以期待它们在未来几个世纪继续保持重要性。

52.答案 D细节题展开解析
判型

What do we learn about much of the popular music currently produced? 是对某具体对象的客观信息提问，不带 infer/imply，按细节题处理。

拆句

much of the popular music currently produced 是名词短语：much of 限定范围（大部分），currently produced 是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 popular music = 当下被制作出来的流行音乐。后置分词定语是四级长难句高频结构，识别它能避免把 produced 误读成谓语。

定位
P2

The most major indicator of classical music's importance in society today is the fact that much of the popular music that is currently being produced uses similar beats, harmonies, and melodies(旋律) as those that were used in some of classical music's best works.

古典乐在当今社会重要性的最显著标志是：当下被制作出来的大部分流行乐使用了与古典乐一些最佳作品相似的节拍、和声与旋律。

选项
A偷换概念

原文说的是「不学乐理的人难以察觉这一现象」，没说流行乐让古典乐迷难以欣赏。

B过度推断

原文只说流行乐「使用相似要素」，并未称其是古典乐的 refinement（精炼/提升）。

C张冠李戴

原文是「交响乐对社会音乐品味的影响是 subtle 的」，主语是交响乐的影响、不是流行乐。

D✓ 正确
uses similar beats, harmonies, and melodies(旋律) as those that were used in some of classical music's best works↔It contains elements similar to those in some masterpieces of classical music

选项 D 把 similar 与 some of classical music's best works 都原样保留，仅把并列名词上位为 elements，是定位句的标准同义替换。

54.答案 B细节题展开解析
判型

题干 Why are classical music pieces still used as background music in modern movies? 是对一个具体事实问原因，答案在原文有直接对应，按细节题处理。

拆句

题干主干 Why are classical music pieces still used as background music ...？被动语态 are used，still 表「仍然」，in modern movies 是地点状语。识别 are used 是被动而非主动是关键，能避免把题理解成「古典乐使用了什么」。

定位
P4

Fans of classical music can also tell you that there are few types of music that are more expressive. So, it should come as no surprise to anyone that classical music pieces are still used as background music in modern movies.

古典乐迷也会告诉你，没有多少类型的音乐比它更具表现力。所以，古典乐至今仍被用作现代电影的背景音乐就一点也不意外了。

选项
A无中生有

原文从未说「电影制作方可选的音乐类型很少」，only 谈古典乐自身的表现力强。

B✓ 正确
there are few types of music that are more expressive↔They are considered to be the most expressive type of music

题干问 why，定位句的 So 前那句被替换为最高级表达，正好回答原因；这是唯一与原因句对应的选项。

C因果颠倒

原文 These are often very well received amongst mainstream music fans 是讲电影配乐的结果——观众接受——并未说这是它被选作配乐的原因。

D张冠李戴

Some of the world's greatest hits have been parts of rock operas 谈的是摇滚歌剧的成就，与电影配乐被使用的原因无关。

判型

What do we learn about our modern music? 是对 modern music 的客观信息提问，按细节题处理；但因对象是全文性概念，定位句多落在尾段总结。

拆句

题干主干 What do we learn about our modern music？our modern music 是介词宾语，about 引出谈论对象。结构简单，无陷阱。

定位
P5

Classical music and opera are the very foundation of what our modern music is based upon.

古典乐与歌剧正是我们现代音乐所赖以建立的根基。

选项
A✓ 正确
Classical music and opera are the very foundation of what our modern music is based upon↔It could not have come into being without classical music and opera as its foundation

选项 A 用「否定 + without」的常见同义改写技法，把肯定陈述变成必要条件，逻辑完全等价，是定位句标准的同义替换。

B无中生有

原文从未拿现代音乐和古典乐/歌剧比对社会影响力的高下，仅强调古典乐和歌剧仍重要。

C偷换概念

原文谈的是「根基」与「重要性」，不是受欢迎程度（popularity）的比较。

D过度推断

原文 we can expect them to continue to remain important for centuries to come 只是肯定古典乐和歌剧的持久重要，没有否定现代音乐重要性。
