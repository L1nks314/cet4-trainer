9 段 · 5 题
P1

New research suggests that pandas may be at risk of dying out because they are too comfortable. Experts say too much happiness can stop the bears from searching for new mates.

P2

Environmentalists have long believed that building roads or homes near the bears may threaten their survival by "reducing or fragmenting their natural: habitats", The Times reported. But the new research suggests that a "modest degree of discomfort and fragmentation" may actually help preserve panda populations.

P3

The research was conducted by scientists from Michigan State University. It concluded that pandas fail to wander off in search of new mates if they find their habitat too comfortable, resulting in a lack of vital genetic diversity.

P4

For their study—outlined in a paper in the journal Conservation Biology—the team looked at genetic diversity and spread among a Chinese panda population. The ideal level of perfectly livable habitat was found to be only 80% of an area, with the remainder either too harsh or too affected by human activity.

P5

The experts concluded that pandas should ideally "be happy enough to thrive, but not so content that they don't want to move around and find new mates".

P6

Their conclusions about what The Guardian described as this "sweet spot" are in line with the so-called Goldilocks principle: that there can be just the right amount of something. The concept has been applied to a wide range of disciplines, from developmental psychology to economics and engineering.

P7

Claudio Sillero, a professor of conservation biology at Oxford University, told the newspaper that the new findings could have implications beyond panda conservation.

P8

"Most large animals that eat meat live in increasingly fragmented landscapes," said Sillero, who was not involved in the research. "It may well be that the messy nature of their relationship with human efforts induces more animals to scatter or travel further, and might result in greater genetic connectivity and enhanced population persistence."

P9

The most recent count of pandas found that there were more than 1,800 left in the wild, putting them on the list of vulnerable, but not endangered, species.

46.答案 D细节题展开解析
判型

What do we learn from new research about pandas? 问 "new research" 直接告诉了我们什么，是对研究结论原文的客观转述题。

拆句

题干 Q46 是一个标准 wh- 问句：What 作 learn 的宾语，from new research 是来源状语，about pandas 修饰 research，限定研究对象。结构平铺直叙，无陷阱。

定位
P1

New research suggests that pandas may be at risk of dying out because they are too comfortable.

新的研究表明，熊猫可能因为生活得太舒适而面临灭绝的风险。

选项
A张冠李戴

losing habitat due to the building of roads and houses 是 P2 中 environmentalists "long believed" 的旧观点，并非新研究的结论。

B过度推断

原文是 pandas fail to wander off in search of new mates if they find their habitat too comfortable——条件状语限定，They have stopped seeking new mates for reproduction. 删掉条件、把可能性说成全体熊猫已经停止寻偶，与原文不符。

C与原文相反

新研究主张栖息地适度碎片化反而"helps preserve"熊猫，They may not adapt to the fragmentation of their habitat. 反过来说熊猫不能适应碎片化，与原文相反。

D✓ 正确
may be at risk of dying out↔may cease to exist
because they are too comfortable↔as a result of enjoying too good a life

D 选项把"太舒适 → 可能灭绝"两层语义都对位地复述了一遍，与预判完全等值，可直接锁定。

47.答案 A推断题展开解析
判型

What can we conclude from the new research 中的 conclude 是推断/结论类信号词，要求在原文事实之上做一步合理推导。

拆句

What can we conclude from the new research by scientists at Michigan State University? 中 by scientists at Michigan State University 是介词短语后置修饰 research，限定研究来源；主干 What can we conclude 即 "我们可以得出什么结论"，结构简单。

定位
P3

The research was conducted by scientists from Michigan State University. It concluded that pandas fail to wander off in search of new mates if they find their habitat too comfortable, resulting in a lack of vital genetic diversity.

这项研究由密歇根州立大学的科学家们开展。研究得出结论：如果熊猫觉得栖息地太舒服，它们就不会外出寻找新的配偶，从而导致缺乏关键的遗传多样性。

选项
A✓ 正确
Environmentalists have long believed that building roads or homes near the bears may threaten their survival↔Environmentalists' long-time belief regarding panda conservation
But the new research suggests that a modest degree of discomfort and fragmentation may actually help preserve panda populations.↔may be misleading

A 项正是把 P2 的"旧信念"与 P3 新研究的反向结论合在一起得出的合理推断，与预判完全一致。

B张冠李戴

Housing development near pandas' homes may threaten their survival. 几乎是 P2 旧观点的原文转述，不是新研究的结论。

C无中生有

原文只说舒适让熊猫不愿外出找新伴，未说栖息地"不适合繁衍"；Pandas' natural habitats are becoming less suitable for reproduction. 是无中生有。

D与原文相反

原文是"栖息地碎片化"对熊猫"种群延续"有帮助，而非"碎片化使熊猫数量增加"。The increased panda population is attributed to the fragmentation of their habitat. 把帮助保存说成数量增加，且因果说法不准确，与原文相反。

48.答案 C细节题展开解析
判型

What is the experts' conclusion regarding pandas? 问专家给出的明确结论，是对一段原文的客观复述。

拆句

What is the experts' conclusion regarding pandas? 中 regarding pandas 是介词短语作 conclusion 的修饰语，相当于 about pandas，限定结论的对象；句子是简单系表结构。

定位
P5

The experts concluded that pandas should ideally "be happy enough to thrive, but not so content that they don't want to move around and find new mates".

专家们得出结论：熊猫理想的状态应当是"既能开心地繁衍生息，又不至于舒服到不愿四处走动去寻找新的配偶"。

选项
A无中生有

P5 强调的是"恰好的舒适度"，不是"提供一个理想的栖息地刻不容缓"。It is urgent to provide an ideal habitat for them to thrive. 中 urgent 是原文未提的紧迫态度，且 ideal habitat 把原文复杂的"sweet spot"过度简化。

B张冠李戴

遗传多样性出现在 P3 是机制说明，不是 P5 专家结论的内容。It is very important to preserve their genetic diversity. 直接把别处的细节当作"专家结论"。

C✓ 正确
be happy enough to thrive, but not so content that they don't want to move around and find new mates↔Their chances of finding new mates have a lot to do with their habitat.
their habitat（隐含于 happy/content 之中——"舒适"是栖息地状态）↔their habitat

C 用"栖息地与找新伴几率有关"概括了预判中的两层逻辑，且未做超出原文的推论，是最贴的概括项。

D无中生有

原文只在 P2 提到旧观点中道路住宅可能威胁栖息地，但新研究并未说生存环境"持续在恶化"。Their environment for survival has been continuously worsening. 是无中生有。

49.答案 B推断题展开解析
判型

What can we infer from the passage 中 infer 是推断题的标志词，要求基于原文做一步合理推导。

拆句

What can we infer from the passage about the Goldilocks principle?：about the Goldilocks principle 是介词短语，作 infer 的宾语补足说明，相当于 "关于 Goldilocks 原则"。结构无陷阱。

定位
P6

Their conclusions about what The Guardian described as this "sweet spot" are in line with the so-called Goldilocks principle: that there can be just the right amount of something.

他们关于《卫报》所说的这个"甜点位"的结论，与所谓的 Goldilocks 原则一致：即任何事物都可以有一个恰到好处的量。

选项
A无中生有

It needs to be confirmed by more studies on pandas. 原文未提及；Goldilocks 原则是"被沿用"的概念，不存在"还需进一步研究确认"的语义。

B✓ 正确
Their conclusions about what The Guardian described as this "sweet spot" are in line with the so-called Goldilocks principle↔It applies to the preservation of pandas too.
applied to a wide range of disciplines, from developmental psychology to economics and engineering↔applies to … too

B 用 applies to … too 精确表达了"该原则同样适用于熊猫保护"，是 in line with 一步推出的合理结论。

C与原文相反

原文是熊猫研究印证了原则，不是原则对熊猫研究有启示。It has implications for future panda research. 把作用方向反着写。

D偷换概念

It can be used to locate the right spot for pandas. 把 sweet spot 误当成熊猫的地理 sweet spot；原文的 sweet spot 是"恰好的舒适程度"的比喻，不是物理位置。

50.答案 C细节题展开解析
判型

What can the new findings do according to Professor Sillero? 直接问 Sillero 教授的原话，是对引语原文的客观转述。

拆句

What can the new findings do according to Professor Sillero? 主干是 What can the new findings do，according to Professor Sillero 是介词短语作来源状语；句中 do 是实义动词，被 What 提前作宾语。

定位
P8

It may well be that the messy nature of their relationship with human efforts induces more animals to scatter or travel further, and might result in greater genetic connectivity and enhanced population persistence.

很可能正是它们与人类活动这种"乱糟糟"的关系，促使更多动物分散、走得更远，从而带来更强的遗传连通性，以及种群存续能力的提升。

选项
A以偏概全

Sillero 的话恰恰是说"超出熊猫保护"，Help discover new ways for the conservation of pandas. 把启示范围又拉回熊猫，与原文方向相反。

B张冠李戴

putting them on the list of vulnerable, but not endangered, species 只是说现状已不在 endangered 名单，并非新发现的功效。Help remove pandas from the list of endangered species. 把背景数据当作新发现的功效。

C✓ 正确
implications beyond panda conservation↔Shed light on the conservation of most large meat-eating animals.
Most large animals that eat meat live in increasingly fragmented landscapes↔most large meat-eating animals

C 用 Shed light on + most large meat-eating animals 直接对应原文 implications beyond panda conservation + Most large animals that eat meat，与预判完全一致。

D偷换概念

Sillero 强调 induces more animals to scatter or travel further，给出的是积极效益，不是 Show the complexity of interactions between humans and animals. 那种中性"展示复杂性"。

第 2 篇 · 第 51–55 题

5 段 · 5 题
P1

With those born with natural talents, it feels as if they excel without really trying. But what about those of us who don't have a natural talent? We've been told all our lives that if you work hard, you too can succeed. But with the release of Angela Duckworth's Grit, we are given a new key to success.

P2

"As much as talent counts, effort counts twice," says Duckworth in Grit. She introduces a new concept that talent may be overrated, and if you want real success, what you need is grit, the perfect combination of passion and persistence. Even if you have natural talent, it's nothing without grit.

P3

Duckworth says grit is the difference between success and failure. A person who has grit is more likely to succeed than a person who does not. When we think about attaining success—whether it's landing that job or learning that new skill—our thoughts are immediately burdened by all the things we must first learn. If you want that new job, you have to learn the job skills, then the interview skills, then the dress part—and you must be perfect at all of them. Grit is different because it tells us that perfection isn't the goal.

P4

Grit lifts the unreasonable expectations off our shoulders. Grit tells us that the door is open wider than we first thought possible. Grit allows us to redefine our goals. Think about it: what's something you've always wanted to do, but gave up because you "don't have the skills for it"? What's something you love but aren't good at?

P5

The real workings of grit are to have sustainable passion and continue to try. Effort means more than your natural ability. Even if you haven't mastered a skill, grit tells you that you can still succeed if you can transform your passion into action. In a way, Duckworth is giving new hope to people who have shut the doors on their dreams. She is saying it is possible that you can accomplish anything. If at first you fail, then try one more time with grit.

51.答案 A细节题展开解析
判型

What does the passage say about people born with natural talents 直接问文章对一个具体对象的陈述，没有 infer/imply/mainly about 这类字眼，是典型细节复述题。

拆句

主干是 What does the passage say about people，过去分词短语 born with natural talents 作后置定语修饰 people，结构平铺直叙，无陷阱。

定位
P1

With those born with natural talents, it feels as if they excel without really trying.

对于那些天生有才的人，看上去就好像他们不太费力就能出类拔萃。

选项
A✓ 正确
it feels as if they excel without really trying↔They seem to outdo others without hard work

预判的「看似/不费力/胜过别人」三个语义点在 A 中一一落位，无增项无漏项，A 是定位句的同义改写。

B无中生有

原文只说他们看似不费力就出色，没说他们 know all the secrets to success——「掌握所有成功秘诀」是凭空添加的内容。

C偷换概念

原文是「看上去毫不费力就成功」，选项却说 They feel it only too logical to succeed——「他们觉得自己成功理所当然」是从他们的主观视角描写，与原文「旁观者眼中显得轻松」不一致。

D过度推断

原文只用 it feels as if（似乎）表达印象，选项却用 are bound to（必然、注定）把可能性写成必然性，过度推断。

52.答案 C细节题展开解析
判型

What does Duckworth say about talent 问的是特定人物对特定对象的陈述，是细节题。

拆句

结构与 51 题平行：What does Duckworth say about talent，主谓宾完整，无修饰从句。

定位
P2

As much as talent counts, effort counts twice.

虽然才能很重要，但努力的重要性是它的两倍。

选项
A张冠李戴

It is a new concept much too overrated 把 She introduces a new concept 中「新概念」的所指对象——其实是 grit——硬安到 talent 头上；原文说的「新概念」指 grit，而非 talent。

B与原文相反

原文反复说 talent 不够、需 grit 加持（Even if you have natural talent, it's nothing without grit）；选项却说 talent proves necessary for big achievements，是反向陈述。

C✓ 正确
As much as talent counts, effort counts twice↔It plays a lesser role in one's success
talent may be overrated↔plays a lesser role

预判的「才能不如努力重要、被高估」与 C 的「作用较小」在比较义上完全等值，C 正确。

D与原文相反

原文称 grit 才是 real success 的关键（if you want real success, what you need is grit），选项却说 talent is a guarantee for real success，把被弱化的对象说成「保证」，方向与程度同时反了。

53.答案 B细节题展开解析
判型

What does the passage say about people thinking of attaining success 仍是 say 句型，针对一个具体场景，是细节题。

拆句

主干仍是 What does the passage say about people，现在分词短语 thinking of attaining success 作后置定语修饰 people；其中 attaining 是动名词作 of 的宾语，整体「想着去获得成功的人」。

定位
P3

When we think about attaining success—whether it's landing that job or learning that new skill—our thoughts are immediately burdened by all the things we must first learn. If you want that new job, you have to learn the job skills, then the interview skills, then the dress part—and you must be perfect at all of them.

当我们考虑获得成功时——不管是拿下那份工作还是学会某项新技能——我们的思绪立刻被「必须先学会的所有东西」压得沉甸甸的。如果你想要那份新工作，你得学工作技能，然后学面试技巧，再学穿衣得体——而且你必须在每一项上都做到完美。

选项
A以偏概全

They are puzzled how to present their best to the employer 把原文里「找工作」举例中的「dress part/interview skills」放大成全部困扰，且把 burdened 改成 puzzled——既缩小到一例又改写状态词。

B✓ 正确
our thoughts are immediately burdened by all the things we must first learn↔They are burdened by their expectation of perfection

预判要求「负担 + 完美」两点齐备，B 正好同时命中——burdened 锁住「负担」，expectation of perfection 锁住「完美主义」。

C与原文相反

原文是「想成功的人感到被各种要求压垮」，选项却说他们 will try hard to land a job that fits their skills best——把焦虑负担改写成积极行动，方向反了。

D过度推断

原文说他们意识到要学很多东西，选项却说他们 lacking in all the skills they need——把「要学的东西多」推到「所有技能都欠缺」，all 是绝对化程度词，原文未明说欠缺。

54.答案 D细节题展开解析
判型

How does the author think grit can be helpful to us 问的是 grit 的具体作用，不是隐含推断，是细节题。

拆句

How does the author think grit can be helpful to us 是宾语从句嵌套结构：think 后接省略 that 的从句 grit can be helpful to us，how 修饰整个 think 的方式逻辑——实际问的是「作者认为 grit 以何种方式有帮助」。

定位
P4

Grit lifts the unreasonable expectations off our shoulders. Grit tells us that the door is open wider than we first thought possible. Grit allows us to redefine our goals.

grit 把不合理的期待从我们肩上卸下来。grit 告诉我们这扇门比我们最初以为的开得更宽。grit 允许我们重新定义自己的目标。

选项
A无中生有

原文从未说 grit 让我们 know what we are good at（知道自己擅长什么）；P4 反而提到「你一直想做但因『没技能』就放弃的事」，强调 grit 让你即便不擅长也去做，与「让你知道擅长什么」无关。

B过度推断

原文比喻 the door is open wider than we first thought possible，意思是给我们的可能性变多；选项 opens our eyes to new opportunities 把「门开宽」改成「让我们看见新机会」——「打开眼界」是额外添加的动作，原文只说门更宽、未说我们因此看见了新机会。

C无中生有

原文 Grit lifts the unreasonable expectations off our shoulders 是卸下「必须样样完美」的负担，而非聚焦注意力；选项 focuses our attention on what we do 与「卸担」无关，反而像「让你更专注」。

D✓ 正确
Grit allows us to redefine our goals↔It lets us reconsider the goals to achieve

let / reconsider / goals to achieve 三处都能在原文第三句找到一一对应，D 是 Grit allows us to redefine our goals 的同义改写。

55.答案 B主旨题展开解析
判型

What message does Duckworth try to convey in her book Grit 问的是整本书的核心信息，是典型主旨题。

拆句

What message does Duckworth try to convey in her book Grit：宾语前置 What message，主干 Duckworth try to convey，介词短语 in her book Grit 作地点状语，无难点。

选项
A与原文相反

原文反复说 perfection isn't the goal、Grit lifts the unreasonable expectations off our shoulders，明确否定完美主义；选项却让我们 perfect ourselves to ensure success，方向反了。

B✓ 正确
If at first you fail, then try one more time with grit↔We should stay persistent even in face of failures
grit, the perfect combination of passion and persistence↔stay persistent
continue to try↔stay persistent

B 同时命中预判的两大语义点——「失败」和「坚持」，是把全文主线压缩到一句话的最佳改写。

C以偏概全

Even if you haven't mastered a skill, grit tells you that you can still succeed 恰恰说「没掌握技能也能成」；选项却把全书主旨说成 never master a skill without constant practice——把「关于 grit 的人生观」缩成「学技能要练习」，既偏题又用了 never 这种绝对化词。

D以偏概全
