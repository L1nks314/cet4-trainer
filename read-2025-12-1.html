第 1 篇 · 第 46–50 题

6 段 · 5 题
P1

All living organisms on Earth are exposed to a 24-hour day-night cycle. This cycle is the reason why people rest at night and are active during the day. Consequently, all human body functions also follow this daily rhythm, and the timing of behaviors like exercise or food intake can significantly influence your health. For example, eating at night can lead to weight gain over time because food intake at night leads to increased fat storage.

地球上的所有生物都暴露于24小时昼夜循环中。这种循环是人们在夜间休息并在白天活动的原因。因此，所有人体功能也都遵循这种日常节奏，运动或食物摄入等行为的时间安排会显著影响您的健康。例如，夜间进食会随着时间的推移导致体重增加，因为夜间进食会增加脂肪储存。

P2

Many drug targets in the body follow a 24-hour cycle, too. This means that the specific proteins a drug is designed to modify can react differently to the medicine over the course of a 24-hour time period. Because how the body responds to a drug can differ depending on the time it is taken, it logically follows that taking medicines at specific times could help increase their effectiveness and reduce unwanted side effects.

体内的许多药物靶点也遵循24小时周期。这意味着药物旨在修饰的特定蛋白质可能在24小时内对药物产生不同的反应。由于身体对药物的反应可能因服用时间而异，因此从逻辑上讲，在特定时间服用药物可以帮助提高其有效性并减少不必要的副作用。

P3

When doctors prescribe medicine for people, they rarely consider the best time to take it. There are two main reasons for that oversight. First, many physicians are not aware that some drugs work better during a specific time of the day. And second, most drugs have not been studied for possible different effects during a 24-hour cycle. Therefore, patients are directed to take most drugs during the morning or evening primarily to ensure compliance.

当医生为人们开药时，他们很少考虑服药的最佳时间。造成这种疏忽的主要原因有两个。首先，许多医生不知道某些药物在一天中的特定时间效果更好。其次，大多数药物在24小时周期内没有研究过可能的不同作用。因此，指导患者在早上或晚上服用大多数药物主要是为了确保依从性。

P4

Over 50 years ago, researchers found that the cholesterol drug simvastatin is more effective at lowering cholesterol levels when taken at night rather than during the day. This is because the liver enzyme(酶) these drugs target is more active at night.

50多年前，研究人员发现，胆固醇药物辛伐他汀在夜间服用比白天服用更有效地降低胆固醇水平。这是因为这些药物靶向的肝酶（酶 ）在夜间更活跃。

P5

Taking medicine at the wrong time can even cause harm. My colleagues and I wondered whether midazolam, the most common sedative(镇静剂) used in surgical procedures worldwide, might interfere with the internal clock that protects the heart at night. Currently, there are no guidelines regarding when midazolam should be administered.

在错误的时间服药甚至会造成伤害。我和我的同事们想知道咪达唑仑（全世界外科手术中使用的最常见的镇静剂镇静剂）是否会干扰夜间保护心脏的内部时钟。目前，没有关于何时服用咪达唑仑的指南。

P6

More research is needed to determine the best times to administer treatments for different diseases. I believe taking drug timing into account could help make treatments more effective and help more people worldwide.

需要更多的研究来确定对不同疾病进行治疗的最佳时间。我认为，考虑用药时机有助于使治疗更有效，并帮助全世界更多的人。

46.答案 A细节题展开解析
判型

What do we learn from the passage about ... 问的是文章就「行为时机」明说了什么，答案应能在原文直接找到对应句，不需推断。

拆句

题干 What do we learn from the passage about the timing of our behaviors? 是一个 what 引导的特殊疑问句，主干是「我们从文中得知什么」，about 短语把范围锁定在「我们行为的时机」上。结构平铺直叙，无从句、无否定，唯一要抓的是 about 后面的话题词，它指明这道题只问「行为时机」相关的内容。

定位
P1

Consequently, all human body functions also follow this daily rhythm, and the timing of behaviors like exercise or food intake can significantly influence your health.

因此，人体的所有功能也都遵循这一日常节律，而锻炼、进食等行为的时机会显著影响你的健康。

选项
A✓ 正确
can significantly influence your health↔It has a considerable impact on our health

预判「行为时机大大影响健康」与 It has a considerable impact on our health 完全吻合，故锁定该项。

B偷换概念

原文是「昼夜周期导致行为有节律」，It confines us to a 24-hour day-night cycle 却说「行为时机把我们限制在 24 小时周期里」，把因当成了果，与原文 This cycle is the reason why people rest at night and are active during the day 相悖。

C张冠李戴

It requires us to follow a particular rhythm 说「行为时机要求我们遵循某节律」，但原文里「遵循节律」的主体是人体功能（all human body functions also follow this daily rhythm），并非由「行为时机」提出要求。

D过度推断

It holds the key to all human body functions 称行为时机「掌握着人体所有功能的关键」，原文只说人体功能遵循节律、行为时机影响健康，从未说时机决定一切功能，all 与 holds the key 属夸大。

47.答案 C细节题展开解析
判型

What does the author say about the proteins 问作者对蛋白质的明确陈述，答案落在讲蛋白质的那句话里，属客观查找而非推断。

拆句

题干 What does the author say about the proteins in our body? 主干是「作者关于我们体内的蛋白质说了什么」，say about 后面的 the proteins in our body 是话题。注意四个选项都是完整句子，需要逐个判断哪句和原文一致，题干本身无陷阱。

定位
P2

This means that the specific proteins a drug is designed to modify can react differently to the medicine over the course of a 24-hour time period.

这意味着，药物所要作用的那些特定蛋白质，在 24 小时的进程中对药物的反应可能各不相同。

选项
A与原文相反

They can modify the effects of medicines in different ways 说「蛋白质改变药物的效果」，但原文是药物去作用（modify）蛋白质（the specific proteins a drug is designed to modify），施受关系正好相反。

B张冠李戴

They can reduce unwanted side effects of certain medicines 把「减少副作用」说成蛋白质的能力，可原文里减副作用的是「按特定时间服药」这一做法（taking medicines at specific times could help increase their effectiveness and reduce unwanted side effects），与蛋白质无关。

C✓ 正确
can react differently to the medicine over the course of a 24-hour time period↔Their reaction to medicines changes during the day-night cycle

预判「蛋白质对药反应随时段变化」正是 Their reaction to medicines changes during the day-night cycle 所述，锁定该项。

D无中生有

Their design determines how differently they react to medicines 称「蛋白质的设计决定其反应差异」，原文从未谈蛋白质有何 design，更没说设计决定反应，纯属臆造。

48.答案 A细节题展开解析
判型

What do doctors do when prescribing medicine 问医生实际做了什么，是可在原文直接核对的事实。

拆句

题干 What do doctors do when prescribing medicine for people? 主干「医生开药时做什么」，when prescribing 是时间状语（现在分词作状语，相当于 when they prescribe）。结构清晰，关键是抓 doctors 与 prescribing 这一动作场景。

定位
P3

When doctors prescribe medicine for people, they rarely consider the best time to take it.

医生给人们开药时，很少考虑服药的最佳时间。

选项
A✓ 正确
they rarely consider the best time to take it↔They give little thought to the time of taking it for maximum effect

预判「医生很少考虑最佳服药时间」与 They give little thought to the time of taking it for maximum effect 一致，锁定该项。

B偷换概念

They rarely consider which medicine works better for which patient 把疏忽的对象换成「哪种药对哪个病人更好」，而原文疏忽的是服药「时间」，话题被悄悄换掉。

C偷换概念

They tell patients its possible side effects during a period of 24 hours 说医生告知 24 小时内的副作用，原文讲的是药物未被研究其昼夜差异（most drugs have not been studied for possible different effects during a 24-hour cycle），并非医生告知副作用。

D偷换概念

They tell patients to comply with the directions of drug manufacturers 说让病人遵从药厂说明，但原文 compliance 指病人遵从医嘱按时服药（patients are directed to take most drugs during the morning or evening primarily to ensure compliance），并未提 drug manufacturers。

49.答案 B细节题展开解析
判型

Why do doctors advise ... 问的是原文已经给出的理由，定位到目的状语即可作答，不需自己推因果。

拆句

题干 Why do doctors advise patients to take most drugs in the morning or in the evening? 是 why 引导的因果问句，主干「医生为何建议病人在早上或晚上服大多数药」。advise sb. to do 是「建议某人做某事」结构。问 why 意味着答案是原文里这一建议背后的目的或原因。

定位
P3

Therefore, patients are directed to take most drugs during the morning or evening primarily to ensure compliance.

因此，病人被指示在早上或晚上服用大多数药物，主要是为了确保遵医服药。

选项
A无中生有

To discourage them from making complaints 说目的是「阻止病人投诉」，原文目的只有 ensure compliance，从未提 complaints，凭空捏造。

B✓ 正确
to ensure compliance↔To ensure they take the drugs as instructed

预判「为确保病人按医嘱服药」与 To ensure they take the drugs as instructed 同义，锁定该项。

C与原文相反

To comply with new research findings strictly 说目的是严格遵循新研究成果，但原文恰恰指出多数药未做昼夜研究（most drugs have not been studied），医生让早晚服药并非依据研究，方向相反。

D与原文相反

To guarantee the maximum effect of the drugs 说目的是保证药效最大，但全文论点正是医生没考虑增效时机（they rarely consider the best time to take it），早晚服药并非为了药效最大化，正好相反。

50.答案 C主旨题展开解析
判型

What does the author suggest near the end 问作者在收尾处的整体主张，需要把末段的呼吁概括出来，而非抓某个孤立细节。

拆句

题干 What does the author suggest near the end of the passage? 主干「作者在文章接近结尾处建议了什么」，suggest 在此是「建议、主张」，near the end of the passage 是地点状语，明确把答案锁在文章末段。这是一道带位置提示的建议题。

选项
A以偏概全

Considering drug-taking timing when prescribing drugs for patients 是第三段对医生开药的观点延伸，而题干问的是 near the end 的建议；末段真正的呼吁是「做更多研究」，而非这条局部主张。

B无中生有

Making treatments less complex by taking drug timing into account 称考虑服药时机可让治疗「不那么复杂」，原文末段只说能让治疗更有效、惠及更多人（could help make treatments more effective and help more people worldwide），从未提 less complex。

C✓ 正确
More research is needed to determine the best times to administer treatments for different diseases↔Conducting more studies to find out the best timing for treating different diseases

预判「需更多研究找最佳给药时机」与 Conducting more studies to find out the best timing for treating different diseases 完全对应，锁定该项。

D偷换概念

Finding out the most effective drugs for treating diseases through further research 说要研究「最有效的药物」，可末段研究的目标是「最佳给药时间」（the best times to administer treatments），全文谈的是时机而非药物本身，对象被偷换。

51.答案 A细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What do we learn from the passage about the economics professor and her hairdresser 锁定两位具体人物，要在原文里抓他们各自的具体说法，属于事实型细节题。

拆句

句子主干是 What do we learn from the passage about ...，about 引出涉及的对象；并列结构 the economics professor and her hairdresser 由 and 连接两个名词短语，无从句、无否定，结构平直无陷阱。

定位
P1

The economist said she plans to continue working because she wants to. The hairdresser agreed but for a different reason: She needs the money.

经济学家说她打算继续工作，因为她想这样做。理发师也同意继续工作，但理由不同：她需要钱。

选项
A✓ 正确
The hairdresser agreed but for a different reason↔They differ in their reasons for continuing to work

预判强调 "两人继续工作的理由不同"，A 项 They differ in their reasons for continuing to work 恰好对此点 100% 命中，无引申无超纲，可直接锁定。

B答非所问

B 项 They are both committed to working into their 70s 把题目焦点偏到 "是否都坚持工作到 70 多岁"，但题目问的是 "对照两人有何不同"，不是问共同点。

C无中生有

C 项 They are happy about their current financial situation 说两人都对当前财务状况满意，但理发师明确说 She needs the money，怎么也谈不上 happy。

D偷换概念

D 项 They hold different views about postponing retirement 似乎与 A 相近，但 different views about postponing retirement 意为 "在是否推迟退休这件事上观点不同"，而原文里两人都决定继续工作，差异在理由而不在观点。

52.答案 C细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What is one of the reasons for people to delay retirement 询问具体原因，要在原文清单里点对清单项，属典型细节题。

拆句

主干 What is one of the reasons，后接介词短语 for people to delay retirement 作后置定语；for 引导原因方向，不定式 to delay 表逻辑动作，结构清晰，无难点。

定位
P3

In addition to people being healthier and living much longer, economists say that a combination of financial considerations such as years of slow rise in real wages and a shift away from traditional pensions in the private sector are some of the reasons people delay retirement.

除了人们更健康、寿命更长之外，经济学家说，多重经济因素的叠加——例如实际工资多年增长缓慢，以及私人部门远离传统养老金——是人们延迟退休的部分原因。

选项
A偷换概念

原文说 the increase in the number of people working in less labor-intensive occupations——是 "从事劳动强度小的职业的人变多"，A 项 less intellectually challenging jobs 偷换为 "智力上挑战较小的工作"，labor-intensive（体力密集）与 intellectually challenging（智力挑战）不是一回事。

B无中生有

B 项 The number of jobs suitable for older people has increased in the private sector 说私人部门里适合老年人的岗位增多，但原文 a shift away from traditional pensions in the private sector 谈的是养老金机制远离传统模式，并未谈岗位数量的变化。

C✓ 正确
years of slow rise in real wages↔The rate of wage increase in terms of purchasing power has slowed down for years

预判锁定 "实际工资多年涨得慢"，C 项 The rate of wage increase in terms of purchasing power has slowed down for years 把同一信息用购买力的角度表达，是教科书式同义替换。

D偷换概念

原文 a shift away from traditional pensions in the private sector 是 "私人部门正在远离传统养老金"——指制度变化；D 项 find it hard to rely on traditional pensions 偷换成 "人们觉得难以依赖传统养老金"——指主观感受。两者方向虽近，但原文从未谈及人们的主观难度感。

53.答案 D细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What is the general trend in people delaying retirement 询问的是原文里已经写明的整体规律，去找总括句即可，不需越级推断。

拆句

主干 What is the general trend，介词短语 in people delaying retirement 中 people delaying retirement 是 in 的复合宾语（动名词短语带逻辑主语），结构平直、无从句。

定位
P5

The overall trend is hitting Americans of all different levels of educational attainment, although the percentages vary by category. The share of Americans with bachelor's degrees who were working into their 70s reached nearly 20% in 2018. For those with a high school degree or less, the proportion of those working in their 70s had risen to around 10%, while those with some college education were in the middle at around 15%.

这一整体趋势影响着所有学历层次的美国人，尽管各类别的比例有所不同。拥有本科学历、且仍在 70 多岁工作的美国人比例在 2018 年达到近 20%；高中及以下学历者比例约 10%；有部分大学经历者居中，约 15%。

选项
A无中生有

A 项 The higher their earnings, the more likely they are tempted to delay retirement 把维度换成 earnings（收入），原文 all different levels of educational attainment 谈的是学历，没有任何收入分层数据。

B张冠李戴

B 项 Those who have more job satisfaction tend to retire later than those who have less 谈职业满意度的影响，但本段只有学历分层数据，没有关于 job satisfaction 的对比；此话题虽在第 6 段简短出现，与 "general trend" 不是同一议题。

C张冠李戴

C 项 More men than women are likely to stay in the labor force until their late seventies 涉及性别差异，原文虽然第 6 段提到 particularly for men，但那是讲孤独感、不是 general trend 数据；本段从未做性别对比。

D✓ 正确
The share of Americans with bachelor's degrees who were working into their 70s reached nearly 20%↔The higher their educational level, the more likely they are to delay retirement

预判出 "学历越高，延迟退休比例越高"，D 项 The higher their educational level, the more likely they are to delay retirement 正是这一规律的语句化，无遗漏无超纲。

54.答案 B细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What is the chief reason for college-educated workers delaying retirement 询问原文中经济学家明确给出的主因，按细节题处理即可。

拆句

主干 What is the chief reason，介词短语 for college-educated workers delaying retirement 作后置定语，分词 delaying 修饰 workers 表正在延迟；末尾 according to an economist 是插入限定信息源，结构清晰。

定位
P6

He suggests that many college-educated workers are choosing to stay in the labor force more for social benefits than for financial reasons.

他指出，许多受过大学教育的劳动者选择继续留在劳动力市场，更多是出于社交方面的好处，而不是经济原因。

选项
A与原文相反

A 项 Enjoying financial security 把主因说成 "享受经济保障"，但原文明确 more for social benefits than for financial reasons —— financial 恰恰是被否定的次要原因，A 项正好反向。

B✓ 正确
more for social benefits than for financial reasons↔Staying connected socially

预判抓 social benefits，B 项 Staying connected socially 正面命中；其余选项均与 social 无直接对应，可一眼锁定。

C过度推断

C 项 Contributing more professionally 说 "在专业上贡献更多"，原文 in professional occupations where they tend to enjoy their work more 只说他们多从事专业职业、并享受工作，并未提 "为了贡献更多" 才不退休。

D无中生有

D 项 Increasing social security payments 把 social 与 financial 拼成 "提高社会保障金"，是基于熟词 social 的乱凑，原文从未提及社会保障金（social security 是美国福利专名）。

55.答案 D细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What does the passage say about people doing manual work 直接问原文对该群体的具体说法，按细节题处理。

拆句

主干 What does the passage say about ...，about 引出对象；现在分词短语 doing manual work 后置修饰 people，相当于 who do manual work，无从句无难点。

定位
P7

Workers in more physical jobs, meanwhile, may be more likely to look forward to retiring, Baily says, suggesting those who stay on are more likely doing so for financial reasons.

另一方面，从事体力劳动较多的工人则可能更期待退休；Baily 说，那些继续留下来的多半是出于经济原因。

选项
A过度推断

A 项 They are eager to enjoy life after retiring 把原文 "期待退休" 引申到 "渴望享受退休后的生活"，原文只说期待退休、未提退休后做什么，引申了一步。

B过度推断

B 项 They are likely to have financial troubles 把原文 those who stay on are more likely doing so for financial reasons 推到 "会陷入经济困境"，但 for financial reasons 只说 "出于经济原因"，不等同于 financial troubles。

C无中生有

C 项 They generally don't enjoy doing it 说 "他们通常不喜欢做这工作"，但原文只说他们更期待退休、并未直接评价是否喜欢工作；不能由 "期待退休" 直接等同 "不喜欢工作"。

D✓ 正确
may be more likely to look forward to retiring↔They tend to anticipate retirement

预判抓 "期待退休"，D 项 They tend to anticipate retirement 是 look forward to retiring 的标准英英同义替换，选定无误。
