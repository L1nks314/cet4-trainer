短文一。
Passage 1.
更广泛的互联网接入与医疗、教育和经济发展的改善直接相关。
Greater internet access correlates directly with improved health care, education, and economic development.
然而，生活在农村地区的人在上网方面却落在后面，这限制了他们获得政府服务、银行服务和就业机会。
People living in rural areas, however, lag behind in online use, which limits their access to government services, banking, and job opportunities.
这一挑战在非洲最为明显。
Nowhere is this challenge clearer than in Africa.
大多数非洲人生活在难以铺设互联网线路的农村地区。
Most Africans live in rural areas that are tough to wire for internet access.
现在，一些手机公司正设法把可上网的手机引入非洲市场。
Now, some phone companies are trying to introduce internet-ready phones into African markets.
一些公司已经开始以仅 20 美元的价格出售简易智能手机。
Certain companies have started selling simple smartphones for only $20.
此前，最低价格大约在 40 美元。
Previously, the lowest price had been around $40.
对许多人来说远远买不起。
Well out of reach for many people.
这些设备运行的是来自电子巨头 KaiOS 科技公司的软件。
These devices are powered by software from the giant electronics company KaiOS Technologies Ltd.
大多数公司都在努力让手机功能越来越强大，但 KaiOS 却反其道而行。
Most companies are trying to make phones ever more powerful and capable, but KaiOS went the other way.
它尽力保留智能手机的基本功能，同时削减成本、延长续航，以服务那些可能供电不足的人。
It made every effort to keep the essential capabilities of smartphones, but strip out costs and preserve battery life for people who likely have inadequate access to electricity.
KaiOS 设备为那些许多非洲人仍买不起、并加剧数字鸿沟的昂贵机型提供了一种替代选择。
The KaiOS devices offer an alternative to the more expensive models that remain out of reach of many Africans and contribute to the digital divide.
KaiOS 手机的机身极其基础。
The body of KaiOS phones is as basic as it gets.
它们没有触摸屏，而是用老式的实体键盘操作。
Instead of a touchscreen, they're controlled with an old-school keypad.
它们是为 3G 网络设计的，因为 4G 信号覆盖不到非洲三分之二的用户。
They're designed for 3G networks because 4G coverage doesn't reach two-thirds of Africa's customers.
总的来看，KaiOS 手机所用的零部件大约价值 15 美元，而苹果最高端的 iPhone 零部件价值 390 美元。
In total, KaiOS phones are made from about $15 worth of parts, while Apple's top-of -the-line iPhone has $390 worth of stuff.
短文二。
Passage 2.
多年来，用再生塑料制造塑料制品一直很便宜。
For years, using recycled plastic to make plastic products was cheap.
相比之下，化石燃料塑料则更贵。
By contrast, fossil fuel plastic was more expensive.
因此，可持续的选择在经济上也是划算的选择。
Thus, the sustainable option was an economic option too.
但如今，对大型制造商来说使用新塑料反而更便宜。
But now, it is cheaper for major manufacturers to use new plastic.
根据最近的一份商业报告，与新塑料相比，再生塑料现在每吨要贵 72 美元。
According to one recent business report, recycled plastic now costs an extra $72 a ton compared with newly made plastic.
这可能是因为消费者的需求。
This may be because of consumer demands.
他们正推动在新产品中使用更多再生塑料。
They are pushing for more recycled plastics in new products.
与此同时，新塑料正变得越来越便宜。
Meanwhile, new plastic is becoming cheaper.
这是由于美国石油化工生产的繁荣。再生塑料涨价可能让可持续制造商每年多支出 2.5 亿美元。
This is because of a boom in petroleum chemical production from the U.S. The price increase of recycled plastic could cost sustainable manufacturers an extra $250 million a year.
规模较小的制造商也可能被迫使用新塑料以降低成本。
Smaller manufacturers may also be forced to use new plastic to reduce costs.
透明塑料瓶的制造商也可能为了省钱而选择新的化石燃料塑料。
Makers of clear plastic bottles may also opt for new fossil fuel-based plastic to save money.
塑料包装制造商正受到压力，被要求使用更多再生塑料。
Plastic packaging makers are being pressured to use more recycled plastic.
这样做的目的是希望减少海洋中大量的塑料污染。
This is done in hopes of reducing the enormous amount of plastic pollution in the oceans.
英国政府计划对产品中再生塑料使用比例不足 30% 的公司征税。
The U.K. government plans to tax companies which don't use at least 30% recycled plastic in their products.
此外，政府还计划增加市场上再生塑料的数量。
Additionally, the government is planning to increase the quantity of recycled plastic in the market.
这可能意味着对新回收厂的激励措施。
This could mean incentives for new recycling plants.
此外，地方议会层面可能会改善回收设施，也可能进口再生塑料。
Additionally, recycling facilities may be improved at a local council level and recycled plastic could be imported.
这将有助于增加流通中的再生塑料数量。
This would help increase the amount of recycled plastic in circulation.
短文三。
Passage 3.
在曼哈顿一家名为梦幻屋的商铺里，小睡是可以出售的。
At the Dreamery, a business in Manhattan, naps are for sale.
在一个安静、昏暗的隔间里小睡 45 分钟，收费 25 美元。
A 45-minute session in a darkened enclosure with peace and quiet costs $25.
说明白点，这家机构可算不上酒店。
To be clear, this institution is no hotel.
这是一家小睡馆。
This is a nap joint.
它既出售小睡本身，也出售小睡的理念。
It sells the idea of the nap as much as the nap itself.
一次小睡值 25 美元吗？
Is a nap worth $25?
答案显然是值得。
The answer is obviously yes.
在这一点上，按惯例我该把那些证明小睡好处的所有研究都搬出来。
Here, at this point in the argument, it's traditional for me to bring up all the studies that show the benefits of napping.
但你真的需要专家来告诉你吗？
But do you really need experts to tell you that?
只要看看下午 2 点半你周围的世界就知道了。
Just look at the world around you at 2.30 in the afternoon.
我在家工作已经超过 10 年了，我能完成工作的质量和数量直接取决于我的专注能力。
I've been working from home for more than 10 years now and the quality and quantity of work I can do emerges directly from my ability to concentrate.
我不明白人们不睡午觉怎么还能从事有创造力的职业。
I don't understand how people have creative careers without napping.
每天下午 1 点左右，每个人都会面临同样的选择。
Every day at about 1 p.m., everyone faces the same choice.
睡到下午 2 点然后工作到 5 点，或者发白日梦、在社交媒体上闲逛、参加毫无意义的会议直到晚上 7 点。我那些还在办公室上班的朋友告诉我，他们的老板坚持要他们选择第二种，还把小睡和懒惰联系在一起。
Sleep until 2 p.m. and then work until 5, or daydream and drift around social media and attend pointless meetings until 7 p .m. The friends I have who still work in offices inform me their bosses insist that they take the second option and that napping is associated with laziness.
我真觉得这很怪，因为如果你小睡得当，那就像睡了一整夜后醒来一样，你能让一天的专注力翻倍。
I genuinely find it odd, for if you nap properly, it's like waking up from a full night's sleep and you can double your day's worth of concentration.
