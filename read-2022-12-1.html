第 1 篇 · 第 46–50 题

6 段 · 5 题
P1

To write his 2010 book, The 5-Factor World Diet, nutritionist Harley Pasternak traveled to the healthiest countries around the world to learn more about what made their meals extra nourishing.

营养学家哈雷·帕斯捷尔纳克（ Harley Pasternak ）为了撰写他在2010年出版的《五因素世界饮食》（ The 5-Factor World Diet ）一书，前往世界上最健康的国家，了解是什么让他们的膳食变得更加营养。

P2

He noted that Japanese people ate a wonderful variety of seaweeds, and that Chinese people tried to include at least five different colors in every meal. Pasternak also came away with some valuable observations about how different the North American way of life was, compared with many other countries.

他指出，日本人吃各种各样的海藻，中国人试图在每顿饭中至少加入五种不同的颜色。帕斯捷尔纳克还就北美的生活方式与其他许多国家相比有多么不同提出了一些有价值的观察。

P3

For starters, Americans eat much bigger portions than people in other countries. "We don't prioritize eating seasonally or locally, and we also add lots of salt, sugar and thickening agents to our foods," explained Pasternak. Contrast that to the healthy Mediterranean, Nordic and Okinawan diets listed in Pasternak's book. They all seem to stick to the ethos(特质) of regional, seasonal produce.

首先，美国人吃的比其他国家的人多得多。帕斯捷尔纳克解释说： “我们不会优先考虑季节性或当地的饮食，我们还会在食物中添加大量的盐、糖和增稠剂。”这与帕斯捷尔纳克书中列出的健康地中海、北欧和冲绳饮食形成鲜明对比。他们似乎都坚持当地季节特质性农产品的精神。

P4

For example, a traditional Mediterranean diet includes fruits, vegetables, whole grains, nuts and olive oil as the main components of nutritional intake. Fish, chicken and red wine make moderate appearances, while red meat, salt and sugar are used much less often. The benefits of a traditional Mediterranean diet have been studied since the 1970s, and researchers have found that living that olive oil life can help people lose weight, lower their heart disease risk and reverse diabetes.

例如，传统的地中海饮食包括水果、蔬菜、全谷物、坚果和橄榄油作为营养摄入的主要成分。鱼、鸡和红葡萄酒外观适中，而红肉、盐和糖的使用频率要低得多。自20世纪70年代以来，人们一直在研究传统地中海饮食的好处，研究人员发现，橄榄油生活可以帮助人们减肥、降低心脏病风险和逆转糖尿病。

P5

Most other healthy eating cultures also make meals an event—say, multiple courses around the family table, or a glass or two of red wine at a long lunch—as opposed to hastily wolfing down handfuls of cereal above the kitchen sink and calling it dinner.

大多数其他健康饮食文化也把吃饭当作一种活动--比如，在家庭餐桌上吃多道菜，或者在漫长的午餐时间喝一两杯红葡萄酒--而不是匆忙地在厨房水槽上吞下几把麦片，称之为晚餐。

P6

Each of the healthy eating cultures has its own unique feature. But Pasternak did take note of one unifying factor in all of the healthy societies he observed. "The only overlapping feature in most of these healthy countries is that they all walk way more than the average American," said Pasternak, "So really, regardless of what you're eating, if someone's walking four miles more than you each day, they're going to be a lot thinner and live a lot longer than you."

每种健康饮食文化都有其独特的特点。但帕斯捷尔纳克确实注意到了他所观察到的所有健康社会中的一个统一因素。帕斯捷尔纳克说： “大多数健康国家唯一的重叠特征是，他们都比普通美国人走得更多，所以真的，无论你吃什么，如果有人每天比你多走四英里，他们会比你瘦得多，寿命也长得多。”

46.答案 A细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What characterizes Japanese and Chinese foods? 是 what 直接询问客观特征，没有 infer/suggest/imply 之类的推断信号，要的就是原文写明的事实。

拆句

题干 What characterizes Japanese and Chinese foods? 是标准的 what 主语疑问句：what 作主语，characterizes 作谓语，Japanese and Chinese foods 是宾语，结构平铺直叙，无陷阱。

定位
P2

He noted that Japanese people ate a wonderful variety of seaweeds, and that Chinese people tried to include at least five different colors in every meal.

他注意到日本人吃种类繁多的海藻，中国人则努力让每餐都包含至少五种不同的颜色。

选项
A✓ 正确
a wonderful variety of seaweeds↔Variety
at least five different colors in every meal↔Variety

预判核心是"多样性"，Variety 既能覆盖 variety of seaweeds，也能覆盖 five different colors，是把两条并列事实的最大公约数提炼出来的上位词，与预判一致。

B无中生有

定位句 He noted that Japanese people ate a wonderful variety of seaweeds, and that Chinese people tried to include at least five different colors in every meal. 通篇讲海藻种类和颜色，没有任何 flavor/taste 的描述；选项 Flavor 在原文里没有对应。

C以偏概全

选项 Color 只对应 at least five different colors in every meal 这一半，根本不能解释 a wonderful variety of seaweeds——日本人吃海藻种类多与颜色无关；以局部冒充整体。

D无中生有

Naturalness 意为"天然性、未加工"，原文定位句没有 natural/unprocessed/raw 之类的词；海藻和颜色多样不等于"自然"。

47.答案 B细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What is typical of Americans in the way of eating? 用 typical 直接问"典型"，要求原文里被作者明确点出的美国人吃饭特征。

拆句

题干主干是 what is typical of Americans，in the way of eating 是修饰 typical 的范围介词短语；结构平铺直叙，无陷阱。

定位
P3

For starters, Americans eat much bigger portions than people in other countries.

首先，美国人吃的分量比其他国家的人要大得多。

选项
A与原文相反

原文 We don't prioritize eating seasonally or locally, and we also add lots of salt, sugar and thickening agents to our foods 说的是美国人"不"重视当季当地、还要加大量盐糖增稠剂——明显是不健康、不重营养的做法，emphasize nutrition 与原文方向相反。

B✓ 正确
eat much bigger portions↔tend to eat quite a lot
than people in other countries↔tend to

预判核心是"美国人吃得多"，They tend to eat quite a lot. 把定位句的数量比较结论直接保留下来，没做引申，与预判完全一致。

C无中生有

原文讲的是分量大、不重视当季当地、爱加盐糖——没出现 convenience/quick/fast/easy 等"便利"语义的词；选项 prioritize convenience 在原文中无依据。

D无中生有

原文谈份量、当季当地、盐糖增稠剂，未涉及 flavor/taste；care about flavors 在原文中没有对应表述。

48.答案 C细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What features are in Mediterranean, Nordic and Okinawan diets? 直接询问几种饮食"具备什么特征"，答案应是原文明写的共同特征。

拆句

题干 What features are in Mediterranean, Nordic and Okinawan diets?：what features 作主语、are 系动词、in Mediterranean, Nordic and Okinawan diets 是表语介词短语，把范围限定在这三种饮食里；结构清晰，无陷阱。

定位
P3

Contrast that to the healthy Mediterranean, Nordic and Okinawan diets listed in Pasternak's book. They all seem to stick to the ethos(特质) of regional, seasonal produce.

把这一点（指美国人的饮食习惯）与 Pasternak 书中列出的健康的地中海、北欧和冲绳饮食对比一下。它们似乎都坚守着"当地、当季食材"这一特质。

选项
A以偏概全

fruits, vegetables 出现在 P4 段对地中海饮食的展开里；但题目问的是三种饮食的共有特征，Fruits and vegetables 只是地中海饮食的成分，不能说北欧和冲绳饮食也以这两样为主——选项错把局部例证当全局共性。

B以偏概全

olive oil 与 red wine 同样出现在 P4 段对地中海饮食的描写里，是地中海饮食的特色，但不是北欧和冲绳饮食的特征；选项把地中海饮食的标志当成三地共同点。

C✓ 正确
regional, seasonal produce↔Seasonal local produce

预判核心"当地、当季、食材"三层在 Seasonal local produce 中一一对应，无遗漏；这是细节题正确项最干净的样子——预判几个要素，选项几乎逐词对接。

D无中生有

原文中谈到三地饮食的共同点只有 regional, seasonal produce（当地当季食材），并未出现 unprocessed/raw/whole 之类强调"未加工"的语义；Unprocessed ingredients 是凭健康饮食的常识印象生造的特征。

49.答案 D细节题展开解析
判型

题干 What do people in most healthy eating cultures have in common? 直问"共同之处"，不含推断信号词，只要找原文的归纳句即可。

拆句

题干 What do people in most healthy eating cultures have in common?：what 作 have 的宾语前置，主语是 people in most healthy eating cultures；have…in common 是固定搭配"有共同点"，结构无陷阱。

定位
P5

Most other healthy eating cultures also make meals an event—say, multiple courses around the family table, or a glass or two of red wine at a long lunch—as opposed to hastily wolfing down handfuls of cereal above the kitchen sink and calling it dinner.

大多数其他健康饮食文化都把吃饭当作一件"大事"——例如全家围桌吃几道菜，或者午餐时悠悠喝上一两杯红酒——而不是匆匆抓把麦片站在水槽边解决一顿。

选项
A以偏概全

get the whole family to eat at a table 只对应 multiple courses around the family table 这一个例子，没覆盖另一例 a glass or two of red wine at a long lunch，更没覆盖"重视用餐"这个主旨；以一个例子冒充整段共性。

B无中生有

原文 P5 只说 long lunch（漫长的午餐），完全没有 regularly/punctually/on time 这类"规律/守时"的语义；选项 eat their meals regularly and punctually 在原文里无对应。

C与原文相反

原文中 handfuls of cereal above the kitchen sink 是用来作"反例"的——讽刺美国人草率吃法的——而选项 consume plenty of cereal for breakfast 反而把这条反例当成健康文化的优点，方向南辕北辙。

D✓ 正确
make meals an event↔attach great importance to their meals

预判核心"重视用餐"与 attach great importance to their meals 字字对应；同时这一表达覆盖了原文后面三个例证（围桌、几道菜、长午餐），是所有例证背后的统一主旨。

50.答案 B细节题展开解析
判型

题干 Compared with the average American, people in healthy societies ___________. 是补全句、内容来自原文的事实性对比陈述，不要求做推断，按细节题走。

拆句

题干 Compared with the average American, people in healthy societies ___________. 是不完整陈述句：Compared with the average American 是过去分词短语作状语，逻辑主语是后面的 people in healthy societies；选项是句子谓语的延续，需保证主谓搭配自然。

定位
P6

The only overlapping feature in most of these healthy countries is that they all walk way more than the average American.

这些健康国家唯一的共同特征是：他们走路都远远多于普通美国人。

选项
A偷换概念

原文 if someone's walking four miles more than you each day 中的 four miles 是 Pasternak 的进一步举例性数字，且是"多走 4 英里"（每天比对方多 4 英里），不是"至少走 4 英里"；选项 walk at least 4 miles a day 把"多走的差值"偷换成"日行的总数"，并加了原文没有的 at least。

B✓ 正确
walk way more than the average American↔do considerably more walking

预判核心"走路远多于美国人"被 do considerably more walking 完整保留——比较级 more、程度副词 considerably（= 原文 way）、活动 walking，三层信息一一对应。

C偷换概念

原文 they're going to be a lot thinner 是"会瘦很多"——这是走路多带来的结果，不是健康社会的人"在意身材"这一主观倾向；选项 pay more attention to body shape 是把客观结果错读成主观态度。

D无中生有

整篇文章谈份量、海藻、五色、当地当季食材、用餐重视度、走路——通篇没出现 organic（有机的）这个词；选项 consume a lot more organic food 在原文中完全无对应。

第 2 篇 · 第 51–55 题

5 段 · 5 题
P1

Recognizing when a friend or colleague feels sad, angry or surprised is key to getting along with others. But a new study suggests that being sensitive to people's feelings may sometimes come with an extra dose of stress. This and other research challenge the prevailing view that emotional intelligence is uniformly beneficial to its bearer.

认识到朋友或同事何时感到悲伤、愤怒或惊讶是与他人相处的关键。但一项新的研究表明，对人们的感受敏感有时可能会带来额外的压力。这项研究和其他研究挑战了普遍的观点，即情绪智力对其持有者是一致有益的。

P2

In a study, psychologist Myriam Bechtoldt of the Frankfurt School of Finance and Management in Germany asked 166 male university students a series of questions to measure their emotional intelligence. For example, they showed the students photographs of people's faces and asked them to what extent feelings such as happiness or disgust were being expressed. The students then had to give job talks in front of judges who displayed serious facial expressions. The scientists measured concentrations of stress hormones in the students' saliva(唾液) before and after the talk.

在一项研究中，德国法兰克福金融与管理学院的心理学家Myriam Bechtoldt向166名男性大学生提出了一系列问题来衡量他们的情商。例如，他们向学生展示人们脸部的照片，并询问他们在多大程度上表达了幸福或厌恶之类的感觉。然后，学生们不得不在评委面前进行工作谈话，评委们表现出严肃的面部表情。科学家们在演讲前后测量了学生唾液中的唾液压力荷尔蒙浓度。

P3

In students who were rated more emotionally intelligent, the stress measures increased more during the experiment and took longer to go back to baseline. The findings suggest that some people may be too emotionally clever for their own good, says Bechtoldt. "Sometimes you can be so good at something that it causes trouble," she notes.

在情绪更聪明的学生中，压力测量在实验期间增加更多，并且需要更长时间才能回到基线。Bechtoldt说，研究结果表明，有些人在情感上可能过于聪明，不符合自己的利益。她指出： “有时候你可以非常擅长一些事情，这会带来麻烦。”

P4

Indeed, the study adds to previous research hinting at a dark side of emotional intelligence. A study published in 2002 in Personality and Individual Differences suggested that emotionally perceptive people might be particularly influenced by feelings of depression and hopelessness. Furthermore, several studies have implied that emotional intelligence can be used to manipulate others for personal gains.

事实上，这项研究增加了之前的研究，暗示了情商的黑暗面。2002年发表在《个性与个体差异》（ Personality and Individual Differences ）杂志上的一项研究表明，情绪敏感的人可能特别受到抑郁和绝望情绪的影响。此外，一些研究表明，情商可以用来操纵他人以获取个人利益。

P5

More research is needed to see how exactly the relation between emotional intelligence and stress would play out in women and in people of different ages and education levels. Nevertheless, emotional intelligence is a useful skill to have, as long as you learn to also properly cope with emotions—both others' and your own, says Bechtoldt. For example, some sensitive individuals may assume responsibility for other people's sadness or anger, which ultimately stresses them out. Remember, as Bechtoldt says, "you are not responsible for how other people feel."

需要更多的研究来了解情绪智力和压力之间的关系在女性和不同年龄和教育水平的人中究竟如何发挥作用。尽管如此， Bechtoldt说，情商是一项有用的技能，只要您学会妥善处理他人和自己的情绪。例如，一些敏感的人可能会对他人的悲伤或愤怒负责，这最终会让他们感到压力。请记住，正如Bechtoldt所说， “你不对他人的感受负责。”

51.答案 C细节题展开解析
判型

What is the finding of the new study?直接问研究的发现，没有 infer/imply/suggest 等推断信号词，答案应能在原文中找到客观对应。

拆句

Q51是一个简单 What is + 主语 + of 短语的疑问句，主干是the finding of the new study，of 引导的介宾短语限定 finding 的归属，无从句、无否定，结构平铺直叙，无陷阱。

定位
P1

But a new study suggests that being sensitive to people's feelings may sometimes come with an extra dose of stress.

但一项新研究指出，对他人情绪敏感，有时会带来额外的压力。

选项
A答非所问

Emotional intelligence helps people get along with others.说的是「情商有助于与人相处」，这是Recognizing when a friend or colleague feels sad, angry or surprised is key to getting along with others.这句旧常识，而不是题干问的the new study的发现。

Emotional intelligence is generally beneficial to its bearer.恰恰是新研究要challenge的主流观点，原文用This and other research challenge the prevailing view that emotional intelligence is uniformly beneficial to its bearer.明确反对它，把它当 finding 是把靶子当结论。
