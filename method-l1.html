2原文 + 连读标注

先做完听写再展开。黄色 = 连读/失爆发生处，也就是你最容易听漏的地方。

Most people believe that building a new habit requires a great deal of willpower.

But according to a growing body of research, that belief may be exactly what holds us back.

A team of psychologists at University College London followed ninety-six adults who were trying to form a new daily routine.

What they discovered was surprising.

The people who succeeded were not the ones with the strongest self-control.

Instead, they were the ones who attached the new behavior to something they already did every day.

This technique is now known as habit stacking.

If you want to start reading before bed, for example, you do not simply promise yourself to read more.

You decide that immediately after you brush your teeth, you will open a book.

The existing habit becomes a kind of signal, and the brain begins to expect the new action without any conscious effort.

The researchers also found that did not damage the process when you miss a single day.

What mattered far more was returning to the routine quickly.

In other words, the key to lasting change is not perfection, but consistency.

3答案与解析

先自己做完再展开，否则等于没做。

展开查看答案与逐题解析
正确答案：1-C　2-B　3-C

第 1 题 → C

原句："The people who succeeded were not the ones with the strongest self-control. Instead, they were the ones who attached the new behavior to something they already did every day."

考点是信号词 Instead。前面先否定 A 选项（willpower / self-control），紧接着用 Instead 给出真实答案。听到 not...Instead... 就等于听到"这道题的答案在后面 3 秒"。

第 2 题 → B

原句："...immediately after you brush your teeth, you will open a book."

同义替换考点：选项 B 的 right after 对应原文的 immediately after。四级听力最爱考这种换词——听到 immediate 就要想 right / at once。
这也是本篇最难的听音点：after you 连读成类似 /ˈɑːftəruː/。如果你把 brush your teeth 听成了别的词，就会直接跑偏。回到第 2 部分把这句跟读 5 遍。

第 3 题 → C

原句："In other words, the key to lasting change is not perfection, but consistency."

同样是 not A but B 结构，答案是 B。而且 not perfection 和 but consistency 两处都是失去爆破，perfection 和 consistency 的实际声音很轻——这里丢分的人，往往是把整个转折结构听漏了。
题型规律：本篇 3 题里有 2 题考 not...instead... / not A but B 的转折结构。四级听力的篇章题，转折词后出答案的概率超过 60%。however / but / instead / in fact / what's more 这几个词，听到就必须竖起耳朵。
