新闻一 全美各地的家庭都满怀忧虑地迎接冬季，因为能源成本正在飙升。
News Report 1 Across the US, families are looking to the winter with fear as energy costs soar.
多种因素正共同造成一个糟糕的局面。
A number of factors are creating a bad situation.
全球能源消费量上升，但供应却减少了。
Global energy consumption is up, but supplies have been reduced.
能源部预计，对于依赖天然气的家庭来说，今年冬天的取暖费将上涨 28%。
The Energy Department projects heating bills will jump 28% this winter, for those who rely on natural gas.
美国近一半的家庭用天然气取暖。
Natural gas is used by nearly half of US households for heat.
预计取暖用油将上涨 27%，电费将上涨 10%。
Heating oil is projected to be 27% higher and electricity 10% higher.
而这一切发生在通胀率上个月加速之际，消费者价格上涨了 6.6%。这是 40 年来最快的增速。
That comes against inflation rates that accelerated last month, with consumer prices growing 6.6%. That's the fastest pace in four decades.
在全国严重依赖取暖用油来为房屋供暖的地区，这种痛苦将尤为剧烈。
The pain will be especially acute in parts of the country that heavily rely on heating oil to keep homes warm.
预计今年冬天，用取暖用油为一户普通家庭供暖将花费超过 2300 美元。
It's projected to cost more than $2,300 to heat a typical home with heating oil this winter.
上个月，联邦政府为低收入家庭能源援助计划追加了 10 亿美元资金，使总额达到 48 亿美元。
Last month, the federal government added $1 billion in funding to Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program, bringing the total to $4.8 billion.
但这比去年超过 80 亿美元的能源援助方案有所减少。
But that represents a reduction from last year's energy assistance package of over $8 billion.
新闻二 许多美国人以为自己的饮食选择很健康，但他们可能对自己的饮食过于乐观了。
News Report 2 Many Americans think they make healthy food choices, but they may be viewing their diet too optimistically.
这是美国农业部研究人员在密西西比州斯通维尔进行的一项新研究的主要发现。
That's the main finding of a new study by researchers at the US Department of Agriculture in Stoneville, Mississippi.
这项研究旨在找出美国人自认为的饮食健康程度与实际情况之间的落差。
The study aimed to identify disconnects between how healthily Americans think they eat and how they actually do.
在这项研究中，研究人员请参与者把自己的饮食评为极佳、很好、好、一般或差。
For the study, the researchers asked participants to rate their diet as excellent, very good, good, fair, or poor.
参与者还填写了 24 小时饮食问卷。
Folks also completed 24-hour food questionnaires.
随后，研究人员对回答进行比较，以了解这两项调查的结果吻合程度如何。
Then, the researchers compared answers to see how well responses to the two exercises matched up.
在超过 9700 人中，约 85% 的人在评价自己饮食质量时判断有误，而且几乎所有都高估了其健康程度。
Of more than 9,700 people, roughly 85 % were off-base when asked to rate the quality of their diet, and almost all overestimated how healthy it was.
那些把自己的饮食评为差的人，判断往往准确得多。
Those who rated their diet as poor tended to be far more accurate.
他们的评分与研究人员的结果十次里有超过九次相符。
Their rating matched that of the researchers more than 9 times out of 10.
新闻三 数十名印度救援人员齐心协力动用起重机，救出困在 20 英尺深井底的一头大象，场面十分暖心。
News Report 3 It was a heartwarming moment that dozens of Indian rescuers rallied together to use a crane in order to save an elephant trapped at the bottom of a 20 -foot well.
这场大规模救援行动上周在一个军营附近展开。
The massive rescue effort took place last week close to an army camp.
这头大象走出丛林，掉进了井里。
The elephant wandered out of the jungle and fell into the well.
军官们听到井里传出声音，意识到有一头大象被困在深处。
The army officers heard a noise coming from the well and realized an elephant was trapped deep inside.
随后军官们给这头动物投了些食物，并向当地林业部门求助。
The officers then threw in some food for the creature and called the local forest department for help.
救援行动的视频显示，救援队如何用起重机把这头庞然大物从井里吊出，放到一辆卡车上。
Video of the rescue operation shows how the team used a crane to lift up the huge animal out of the well and onto the back of a truck.
随后它被送往检查室，评估是否受伤。
It was then taken to an examining room for evaluation of any injuries.
这头大象受到严密看护数天后，被成功放归野外。
The elephant was kept under close supervision for a few days before it was successfully released back into the wild.
