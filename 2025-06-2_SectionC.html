短文一。
Passage 1.
我们以为，如果给顾客提供更多选择，他们就更可能购买我们的产品。
We assume that if we offer our customers more choices, then they'll be more likely to buy our products.
但这就是选择悖论。
But here's the paradox of choice.
如果给一个人提供的选择太多，他们实际上反而不太可能购买。
If a person is presented with too many choices, they are actually less likely to buy.
2012 年，心理学家发表了一项关于果酱的研究。
In 2012, psychologists published a study about jams.
在平常的一天，在一家当地食品市场，人们会看到一个摆着 24 种不同果酱的陈列台。
On a regular day, at a local food market, people would find a display table with 24 different kinds of jams.
然后，在另一天，同样在这家食品市场，人们只能从 6 种不同的果酱中挑选。
Then, on another day, at that same food market, people were given only six different types of jam choices.
猜猜哪个陈列台带来了更多销量？
Guess which display table led to more sales?
研究发现，虽然更大的陈列台引起了更多兴趣，但与较小的陈列相比，人们购买一瓶果酱的可能性几乎低了 10 倍。
The study found that, while the bigger display table created more interest, people were almost ten times less likely to purchase a jar of jam than in the case of the smaller display.
顾客满意度也会受到影响。
Customer satisfaction takes a hit as well.
在这项研究中，较大的果酱陈列比小的陈列导致更低的顾客满意度。
In the study, the bigger display of jams leads to lower customer satisfaction than the smaller display.
在 2015 年发表的一项大规模研究中，研究人员分析了总共 99 项关于选择的研究，特别关注了减少选择有助于提升销量的那些案例。
In an extensive study published in 2015, researchers analysed a total of 99 choice studies and specifically looked at those cases in which reducing choices helped to boost sales.
他们发现了四条促使消费者购买的规律。
They found four rules that motivate consumers to buy.
当人们想快速轻松地做出选择时，当产品比较复杂时，当难以比较各个备选时，以及当消费者没有明确偏好时。
When people want to make a quick and easy choice, when the product is complex, when it's difficult to compare alternatives, and when consumers don't have clear preferences.
所以，如你所见，在选择的方面，少即是多。
So, as you can see, when it comes to choices, less is more.

Passage 2.
新年来了又过去了，许多人可能已经忘了自己 1 月 1 日下定的决心。
The New Year has come and gone, and many may have already forgotten what they had resolved to do on January 1st.

But if that resolution was to volunteer more, now is the best time to start.
假期过后志愿服务往往会减少，到了 2 月，一些组织就缺少了仅一个月前还绰绰有余的志愿者。
Volunteering can drop after the holiday season, and by February, some organizations lack the volunteers they had in surplus just a month ago.
一项调查显示，只有 26% 的美国人参与志愿服务，尽管有报告称许多美国人认为志愿服务很有价值。
According to one survey, only 26% of Americans volunteer, despite reports that many Americans view volunteering as worthwhile.
大多数人把时间和工作安排冲突列为主要的障碍。
Most people cite time and conflicting work schedules as major barriers.
但如果企业把员工志愿服务作为工作的一部分，员工和雇主实际上都会受益。
But employees and employers would actually benefit if companies made employee volunteering part of the job.
研究人员发现，把自己的时间和才能捐献给他人可以降低血压、提升幸福感，并改善身心健康。
Researchers have found donating our time and talent to others can lower blood pressure, boost happiness, and improve mental and physical health.
研究还把员工志愿服务项目与更高的工作效率联系起来。
Studies have also linked employee volunteer programs with more productive employees.
如今人们如此重视员工健康，企业志愿服务项目或许会是 2025 年最完美的决心。
With so much emphasis on employee wellness these days, company volunteer programs could be the perfect 2025 resolution.
但要让志愿服务惠及员工和工作场所，整个组织都需要参与其中。
But for volunteering to benefit employees and the workplace, the entire organization needs to be involved.
允许员工在工作时间投入时间做志愿，工作场所就成了实现个人价值的出口，而不必占用个人时间。
By allowing employees to donate their time during work hours, the workplace becomes an outlet for personal fulfillment without affecting personal time.
在一项调查中，89% 的员工认为，赞助志愿活动的企业营造出了更好的工作环境。
In one survey, 89% of employees perceived companies that sponsored volunteer activities as creating a better working environment.
还有 75% 的人表示，志愿服务对他们的幸福感至关重要。
And 75% said volunteering was critical to their well-being.
短文三。
Passage 3.
英国零售业每年产生的 20 万吨食物浪费中，绝大部分来自超市，但这个问题也影响着独立的奢华食品供应商。

规模很小。
The scale is small.
据估计还不到英国食物浪费总量的 1%。
It's estimated at less than 1% of the UK's food waste total.
但这些供应商的产品质量很高。
But the quality from these suppliers is high.
在当前的食品体系下，从特色奶酪到抢手的咖啡豆，各种东西每天都在被丢弃。
Everything from specialist cheeses to sought-after coffee beans gets thrown away every day in the current food system.
法国企业家索菲·安德烈正是在这种情况下登场的。
This is where French entrepreneur Sophie André comes in.
2016 年，她搬到伦敦，最初创办了一家在全城配送奢华手工食品的公司。
In 2016, she moved to London and began by setting up a business delivering luxury handmade food across the city.
她说，食品配送在这里似乎是件大事。
Food delivery seems to be a big thing here, she says.
于是，我开始骑自行车送货。
So, I started delivering products by bicycle.
在与当地生产商交谈中，我了解到，由于大小或形状的问题，有时会有一小部分产品被浪费掉。
Talking with local producers, I learned that a small part of the production is sometimes wasted due to problems with size or shape.
但这些产品的质量并没有变化。
But the quality of the products remains unchanged.
与近年来涌现的许多食物浪费项目不同，这个想法不只是重新分配被浪费的食物，而是要提供高品质的产品。
Unlike many food waste projects that have sprung up in recent years, the idea is not simply to redistribute waste food, but to provide a high quality product.
安德烈的公司 Elysia 专门为商务会议和活动提供早餐和点心。
André's company, Elysia, offers breakfasts and snacks specifically aimed at business meetings and events.
食材采购回来后，Elysia 用这些本该被浪费的产品制作简单、天然、朴实的餐食和点心。
Once sourced, Elysia uses waste products to create simple, natural, straightforward meals and snacks.
也许与其他被浪费的食物不同，这里的供应很稳定。
Perhaps unlike other waste food, the supply is regular.
供应商总会有一定数量的剩余。
Suppliers will always have a certain amount of surplus.
所以，菜单也能保持稳定。
So, the menus remain regular too.
安德烈相信，这对她的生意和顾客都有好处。
André believes this benefits both her business and her customers.
