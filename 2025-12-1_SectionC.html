6交卷回执

把下面这段发给我（截图或直接复制都行），连同上面的听写照，我立刻给出错因归因和改善方法。

培养一名用户体验设计师所需的技能需要时间、耐心和投入。
Building the skills required of a user experience designer takes time, patience, and commitment.
高等教育是让自己掌握一些核心技能的好途径，但它无法让你为应对客户工作中实际遇到的挑战做好准备。
Higher education is a great way to equip yourself with some core skills, but it will not prepare you for actual challenges you'll face with client's work.
换句话说，熟练使用一款设计工具和几种方法，并不能让你成为一名用户体验设计师。
In other words, being proficient with a design tool and a few methods doesn't make you a user experience designer.
根本不存在一套放之四海而皆准的流程。
There simply isn't a one size fits all process.
要想真正行之有效，需要具备适应能力，而这在学校里其实学不到，更别说在为期 6 个月的训练营里了。
Being effective requires adaptability, something you don't really learn in school, much less in a six month training camp.
这种能力来自工作中的经验积累，以及学会根据项目的需求判断什么是合适的做法。
It's gained through experience on the job and learning what's appropriate given the needs of the project.
我是通过平面设计走上用户体验这条路的，经过许多不同的岗位和经历，才慢慢成为一名用户体验设计师。
I found my way to user experience through graphic design and slowly over many different roles and experiences that led me to become a user experience designer.
要一直在那些我知道能教会我、也能挑战我的团队里谋求职位，需要付出时间和坚持。
It took time and commitment to continue to pursue roles within teams that I knew could teach and challenge me.
这并不是说我认为我的道路是唯一的，但话说回来，任何东西都无法替代真实世界的经验。
That's not to say that I think my path is the only one, but once again, nothing can substitute real world experience.
只要你知道自己的最终目标，并愿意主动寻找机会在过程中学习和成长，你从哪里起步都可以。
You can start anywhere as long as you know your end goal and you commit to actively pursuing opportunities to learn and grow along the way.
短文二，在筹划今年工作时，我们校长问需要做出哪些改变，才能让学生更投入地学习。
Passage two, when planning for this year, our principal asked what needed to change to engage students more in their learning.
我小声回答说「灵活座位」，当时我想到的是我们那时的教室。
I responded in a whisper, flexible seating, thinking about our then current classroom.
那时的教室里是一排排的课桌和蓝色椅子，还有姓名牌提醒每个学生该坐在哪里。
It had rows of desks with blue chairs and nameplates reminding each student where they should be sitting.
这开启了一段旅程，去改变我们的教学实践，也挑战一群六年级学生多年来的学习方式，他们早已习惯坐在一排排的课桌后面。
This began a journey to transform our practice and challenge the ways of learning for a group of year six students used to sitting in desks and rows for years.
灵活座位被定义为使用可移动的家具，来营造一个能激发参与感的学习环境。
Flexible seating has been defined as movable furniture to create an engaging learning environment.
这是教学实践上的一次转变，从以教师为中心转向以学生为中心的学习。
It is a shift in practice from being teacher focused to student focused learning.
对我们来说，灵活座位意味着撤掉大部分传统的椅子和课桌，引入各种不同的座位选择，以提高学生的参与度。
For us, flexible seating has meant removing most of the traditional chairs and desks and introducing a variety of different seating options to promote student engagement.
传统上，采用一排排的座位并对它们稍作改动排成 U 形，是为了最大限度地让学生专注于任务，并减少来自教师的干扰。
The use of rows and their minimal adaptations to U shape were traditionally intended to maximize on task behavior and reduce distraction from the teacher.
教师往往仍在使用这种布局，要么是因为需要管住学生，要么是因为相信教师是教室里最重要的人。
Teachers tend to still use this format because of either the need to control students or the belief that the teacher is the most important person in the room.
现在，我们的学生有一系列不同的座位可选，包括地桌、沙发、凳子、豆袋椅，以及传统的课桌和椅子。
Now, our students have a range of different seating options, including a floor desk, couches, stools, beanbags, and the traditional desk and chair.
从我们目前的经验来看，灵活座位增强了学生对空间的自主感以及对学习的参与度，同时降低了学生疏离感和纪律处分的发生率。
From our experience so far, flexible seating enhances student ownership of space and engagement in learning while reducing rates of student disengagement and disciplinary actions.
这对所有相关的人来说都是好事。
It is a win for all concerned.
短文三，周二，数十名英国学生来学校开始第一天的学习，却因为鞋子问题被打发回家。
Passage three, dozens of British students arriving for their first day of school on Tuesday were sent home over their shoes.
大约 30 名学生被英格兰诺福克的塔弗勒姆中学拒之门外。
About 30 students were turned away from Taverham High School in Norfolk, England.
校长罗杰·哈里斯博士证实，这是因为学校的校服政策发生了改变。
Head teacher, Dr. Roger Harris, confirmed that this was due to a change in the school's uniform policy.
今年出任校长的哈里斯说，他已在 6 月通过电子邮件把更新后的规定通知了家长。
Harris, who became head teacher this year, said he notified parents of the updated rules in an email in June.
校服的价格会让那些在高昂生活成本中挣扎的家庭感到恐慌。
The price of school uniforms can spark panic among families struggling with the high cost of living.
几乎所有英国学校都要求穿校服。
Nearly all British schools have uniforms.
每个上中学的孩子每年平均要花掉家长 337 英镑。
They cost parents an average of £337 per year for each secondary school child.
根据新规定，塔弗勒姆的学生必须穿适合职场场合的正式黑皮鞋。
According to the new rules, students of Taverham are required to wear smart black shoes appropriate for the workplace.
哈里斯认为，加强校服方面的规定能改善学生的学业表现和行为。
Harris held that strengthened rules around school uniforms improved student outcomes and behaviour.
但对一些家长来说，学校的这些限制是不必要的负担。
But for some parents, the school's restrictions are an unnecessary burden.
随着年通胀率攀升超过 10%，许多家庭都预算紧张。
As annual inflation climbs over 10%, many households are on tight budgets.
截至 2022 年 7 月的 12 个月里，英国私人房租价格上涨了 3.2%，是 2016 年以来的最大涨幅。
Private rental prices in Britain rose 3.2 % over the 12 months to July 2022, the largest jump since 2016.
飙升的能源账单让情况更加糟糕。
Soaring energy bills have made things worse.
塔弗勒姆一名学生的母亲露辛达·梅说，她不得不向自己的父母要 65 英镑，来给孩子买一双符合规定的鞋。
Lucinda May, mum of a Taverham student, said that she had to ask her parents for £65 to buy her child the correct pair of shoes.
梅说，学校的校服政策显示出对正在应对高昂生活成本的家长缺乏体谅。
May said that the school's uniform policy showed a lack of regard for parents dealing with the high cost of living.
