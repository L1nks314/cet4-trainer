新闻一。
News report 1.
对 7 岁的塔里克来说，当他把黄油抹到一根玉米上时，一切都变了。
Everything changed for seven-year-old Tariq when he slapped some butter on an ear of corn.
他知道玉米很好吃，但加上黄油就更好吃了。
He knew it tasted good, but butter made it better.
他对农产品的热情让他获得了南达科他州官方玉米大使的身份。
His passion for produce got him a position as South Dakota's official corn ambassador.
塔里克对最爱的蔬菜的表白，为他赢得了「玉米小子」的称号。
Tariq's profession of love for his favorite vegetable earned him the name The Corn Kid.
此前，他的网络采访吸引了数百万次观看，还被改编成了一首歌。
This was after his online interview attracted millions of views and was made into a song.
南达科他州是美国最大的玉米产地之一。
South Dakota is one of the top corn producers in America.
这里的玉米为全球提供食物。
The corn provides nourishment across the globe.
塔里克说，不是每个人都必须喜欢玉米，但每个人都一定要尝一尝，尤其是配上黄油。
Tariq said not everyone has to like corn, but everyone should definitely try it, especially with butter.
塔里克和家人受邀前往南达科他州，参加在该州玉米宫殿举行的荣誉仪式。
Tariq and his family were invited to South Dakota to attend the honorary ceremony at the state's Corn Palace.
官员们希望突出南达科他州的两大产业：旅游业和农业。
Officials wanted to highlight South Dakota's two largest industries, tourism and agriculture.
当塔里克看到这座用玉米建成的宫殿时，简直不敢相信自己的眼睛。
Tariq couldn't believe his eyes when he saw the palace made of corn.
周四宣布了两人被捕的消息。
Two arrests were announced Thursday.
据司法部称，这两人的被捕与马里恩县美国邮政服务信箱发生的一系列邮件盗窃案有关，还涉及持有被盗的邮政钥匙。
The arrests were in connection with a string of mail thefts from U.S. Post Service's collection boxes in Marion County and for the possession of a stolen postal key, according to the Department of Justice.
据一份新闻稿称，23 岁的乔丹·杰克斯和塔罗德·古德曼被捕，因为调查中收集的证据显示，这两人多次用一把邮政钥匙盗窃邮件。
Jordan Jacks and Tarrod Goodman, both 23, were arrested after evidence gathered in an investigation showed the pair stole mail on multiple occasions using a postal key, according to a news release.
杰克斯和古德曼盗窃邮件的时间大约持续了 4 个月。
Jacks and Goodman stole mail for around four months.
调查人员查看了信箱的监控录像。
Investigators reviewed videos of the collection boxes.
录像显示，这两名男子把偷来的邮件装进杰克斯的车里。
The video showed the two men loading the stolen mail into Jacks' vehicle.
调查人员还成功找回了用来打开信箱的那把钥匙。
Investigators were also able to recover the key that was used to open the collection boxes.
就邮件盗窃罪而言，杰克斯和古德曼各面临最高 5 年的联邦监禁，而持有邮政钥匙罪则面临最高 10 年监禁。
Jacks and Goodman each face up to five years in federal prison in the mail thefts and up to 10 years for possession of the postal key.
新闻三。
News report 3.
快时尚能实现可持续吗？
Can fast fashion be sustainable?
研究人员认为可以。
Researchers think yes.
找出快时尚商业模式为何产生浪费，并确定监管机构能否为消费者和制造商建立激励机制，这些都是减少浪费的步骤。
Identifying why the fast fashion business model creates waste and determining whether regulators can establish incentives for consumers and manufacturers are steps to reduce waste.
近来，服装业因其造成带有严重环境后果的浪费问题而受到抨击。
Recently, the clothing industry has come under attack for creating a waste problem with serious environmental consequences.
在缺乏环境上和经济上都可行的回收方案的情况下，快时尚制造商大量生产低质量的衣服，这些衣服只穿几次就被丢弃。
In the absence of environmentally and economically feasible recycling options, fast fashion manufacturers pump out low -quality clothes produced in high volumes that are worn only a few times and then discarded.
研究人员提出了一系列政策建议，从对积压库存进行可持续处理，到征收生产税，以激励制造商和消费者更加重视减少浪费。
The researchers put forth policy contributions ranging from sustainable disposal of leftover stock to production tax to incentivize both manufacturers and consumers to be more waste-conscious.
为了制定有效的政策来遏制服装业对环境的影响，找出供应链中问题的根源很重要。
In order to devise effective policies to curb the environmental impact of the clothing industry, it is important to identify the source of the problem in the supply chain.
这样，制造商、消费者和监管机构就能以有依据的方式认识到快时尚的环境影响，并设计出一套生态系统来减少浪费、激励创新，并创造管理浪费的新商业模式。
Manufacturers, consumers, and regulatory bodies can then take an informed approach to recognize the environmental impact of fast fashion and to design an ecosystem to reduce waste, incentivize innovation, and create new business models to manage waste.
